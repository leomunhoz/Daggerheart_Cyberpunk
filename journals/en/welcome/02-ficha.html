The Edgeheart Sheet is the Daggerheart character sheet with an extra tab, Chrome. New characters start with it (this can be turned off in the module settings), and there is a "New Edgeheart Character" button on the Actors tab.

IN THE HEADER

Next to Hope is the summary d6 | 0: your Humanity Die and your Cyber Load. Click it to open the Chrome tab.

CHROME TAB

  • Humanity: shows your Humanity Die (d4 to d12), with buttons to advance, reduce, and make a Humanity Roll.

  • Cyber Load: adds up the cost of all installed cyberware and shows your chance of staying in control on a Humanity Roll.

  • Installed Cyberware: drag a cyberware from the compendium onto the sheet. The bonuses (HP, Stress, Evasion, Armor, Thresholds) apply on their own; when the cyberware asks for a choice, a "Choose" button appears.

  • Eidolons: drag an Eidolon onto the sheet and use Mobilize: HP, Evasion, Thresholds, Armor, weapons, token, Sync Cost, and Competency Link switch on their own.

  • Competency Access: shows the Competencies unlocked by your class and cyberware, and warns if a card is outside your access.

  • Cyberpsychosis: shows the current state and the Stabilize and End buttons.

AUTOMATIC ON THE SHEET

  • When you roll with Fear with all your Stress marked or with no Hope, the Humanity Roll is made on its own.

  • In Cyberpsychosis, Duality rolls use only the Fear Die as a d20, and features that would require marking Stress can be used without marking it.

  • If the scene ends in Cyberpsychosis, the Humanity Die drops one step.

  • During character creation, the selection window shows only the Edgeheart classes, Life Paths, and Affiliations, and the suggested traits follow the chosen subclass.

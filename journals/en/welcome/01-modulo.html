This module adapts the Edgeheart campaign frame for the Daggerheart system in Foundry VTT, in English and Brazilian Portuguese. Class and subclass names stay in English in both languages (Runner, Breach Specialist, Net Diver...); everything else appears in the chosen language.

GETTING STARTED

  1. Enable the module in your world (Daggerheart system).

  2. To play in Portuguese, also enable the Babele module and choose Português (Brasil) as the Foundry language. Without Babele, the compendiums appear in English.

  3. The compendiums come ready in the Edgeheart SRD folder of the compendium sidebar: Classes, Subclasses, Life Paths, Affiliations, Competency Cards, Weapons, Armor, Cyberware, Consumables, Loot, Adversaries, Environments, Tables, and these Journals.

  4. Create a character: it starts with the Edgeheart Sheet.

WHAT THE MODULE DOES NOT DO

Nothing in the Daggerheart system is modified. The module only uses what the system itself offers: items, effects, hooks, additional sheets, and the system's Homebrew settings (to register the Competencies and the Edgeheart Level Up). A world without the module is not affected.

WHAT IS INCLUDED

  • The 9 classes and 18 subclasses: Runner, Infiltrator, Solo, Augmented, Tech, Broker, Reclaimer, Trauma Doc, and Warden.

  • The 11 Competencies (Network, Ghost, Assault, Chrome, Systems, Influence, Frontier, Medtech, Aegis, Redline, and Blackwall), with 21 cards each, from level 1 to 10.

  • 6 Life Paths and 8 Affiliations.

  • Tier 1 to 4 weapons and armor, 40 loot items, 40 consumables, and the roll tables.

  • The 60 cyberware from the PDF table and the 10 Eidolons.

  • The 8 vehicles, with the Damaged and Disabled states.

  • 25 adversaries and 16 environments.

  • The Edgeheart Sheet, with Humanity, Cyber Load, Cyberpsychosis, Cyberware, Competency Access, and Eidolons.

  • The Crew Sheet, with Reputation and Edge.

  • The Edgeheart Level Up.

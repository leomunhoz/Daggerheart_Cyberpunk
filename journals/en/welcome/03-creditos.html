ORIGINAL CONTENT

Edgeheart — a Cyberpunk and Armored Core-inspired campaign frame for the Daggerheart TTRPG, created by AllenDGray14. This module is an unofficial fan adaptation and translation of that material.

DAGGERHEART

Daggerheart is a TTRPG by Darrington Press. The Daggerheart system for Foundry VTT is maintained by its own team; this module only complements it, without changing it.

THE VOID

The Redline card automations reuse those of the Blood domain from the The Void (Unofficial) module, under the GPL-3.0 license.

ICONS

The content icons come from a cyberpunk-themed icon pack included in assets/cpr. If you distribute the module, check the license of those icons.

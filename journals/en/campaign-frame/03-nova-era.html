Prepare yourself to survive, sell your skills, and decide how much of yourself is still yours.

STARTING POINT

Your story begins with a contract. Perhaps you already know the others. Perhaps you’re strangers brought together by circumstance, debt, opportunity, or desperation.

The details hardly matter. Someone has a job. Someone is willing to pay. And somewhere in the distance, another war is coming.

Edgeheart is inspired by Cyberpunk 2077, Armored Core, Lancer, and many other cyberpunk settings, but it is not bound to any particular canon. Use this campaign frame as a foundation for your own version of the New Dark Age.

Your city may be ruled by corporations, gangs, nations, or something else entirely. The Crash may have happened differently. The Black Wall may hide truths nobody else has discovered.

This future belongs to your table.

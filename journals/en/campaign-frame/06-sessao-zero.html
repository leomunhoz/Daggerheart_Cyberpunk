Ask any of these questions to your players, or create your own.

  • What did your character sell, lose, or replace to become a mercenary?

  • What corporation, gang, nation, cult, or syndicate has a claim on you?

  • What kind of contract would you refuse, no matter the reward?

  • Who in the crew has seen you at your most human?

  • If the world before the Collapse could be restored, would you want it back?

  • What would make you betray a client or the crew?

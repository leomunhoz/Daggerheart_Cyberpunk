WHAT IS THIS CAMPAIGN FRAME?

Edgeheart is a cyberpunk campaign frame for the Daggerheart TTRPG. It reinterprets the themes of corporate oppression, cybernetic augmentation, mercenary contracts, and mechanized warfare into a narrative-driven tabletop experience.

The setting takes place during the New Dark Age, a period following the Crash, when a rogue military AI corrupted the global network and forced humanity to sever nearly all planetary infrastructure. Nations weakened, corporations rose to power, and gangs became the rulers of the streets.

Today, the world survives through isolated networks, corporate influence, black-market technology, and the actions of independent mercenaries willing to risk everything for one more contract.

Mercenaries

You are one of the countless people struggling to survive in the New Dark Age. Some work for corporations, others fight for gangs, or to protect the remnants of nations. Most simply chase the next paycheck. You may become famous, feared, or hunted, but every job carries a price.

Classes and Competencies

In Edgeheart, your character is defined by a Class and a set of Competencies. Your Class represents your role in the world. Competencies determine your specialized training and provide features, equipment, and abilities associated with your role. Together they define how you approach contracts, combat, social encounters, and survival in the New Dark Age.

Cyberware

Cyberware is the foundation of power in Edgeheart. From enhanced eyes and reinforced skeletons to military-grade neural processors and experimental combat systems, cyberware allows ordinary people to perform extraordinary feats.

Every augmentation increases your Cyber Load. The greater your Cyber Load, the closer you move toward Cyberpsychosis. Power always comes at a cost.

Eidolons

Eidolons are advanced mechanized combat platforms recovered from lost technologies beyond the Black Wall. Part weapon, part vehicle, part digital entity, an Eidolon is among the most valuable military assets in existence.

Operating an Eidolon requires specialized implants capable of linking a human mind with an artificial intelligence sophisticated enough to control the machine. Every Eidolon pilot walks a dangerous line between unmatched power and total psychological collapse.

WHAT YOU WILL FIND HERE

  • New Classes representing the most common mercenary archetypes of the New Dark Age, from Netrunners and Assassins to Celebrities and Augmented Soldiers;

  • New Life Paths and Affiliations, replacing Ancestries and Communities to reflect your place in a world ruled by corporations, gangs, and fractured nations;

  • A new equipment system focused on Cyberware, allowing characters to trade pieces of their humanity for power;

  • Advanced combat platforms known as Eidolons, huge and rare military relics capable of turning a skilled mercenary into a walking army;

  • A Humanity and Cyber Load system that tracks the psychological cost of augmentation and the risk of Cyberpsychosis;

  • New weapons, adversaries, environments, factions, and mechanics designed to emulate the themes of cyberpunk dystopias and corporate warfare.

TERMINOLOGY AND MECHANICS

To better represent the setting, several terms are reinterpreted throughout this adaptation. These changes are primarily thematic and help align Daggerheart’s mechanics with a futuristic setting:

  • Magic damage becomes Techno Damage, representing cyberwarfare, advanced weaponry, hostile AI, and technological anomalies.

  • Spellcasting Rolls become Interface Rolls, representing hacking, neural synchronization, digital intrusion, and advanced technological expertise.

  • Domains are reinterpreted as Competencies, specialized fields of training rather than magical disciplines.

If your group decides to play this campaign, share the following before character creation.

YOU ARE MORE THAN YOUR CYBERWARE

Cyberware gives you power, but it is not the whole of who you are. Define the person beneath the chrome.

Your body can be modified, rebuilt, and exploited. But your choices still matter.

Embrace adaptation, but remember that every upgrade has a cost. Some costs are measured in credits. Others are measured in dependency, memory, trust, or humanity.

LOSING YOURSELF IS ALWAYS A RISK

In a world of constant upgrades, replacements, and neural rewiring, the line between who you are and what you’ve become is never stable.

Cyberware enhances you, but it also changes how you think, react, and feel. Push too far, too fast, or too often, and something inside you may begin to fracture.

Cyberpsychosis is not just madness. It is the slow erosion of identity, empathy, and connection. It is the moment when people stop feeling real, when violence becomes easier than understanding, when your own reflection feels distant.

EVERY JOB LEAVES A MARK

Every mission stains something: your reputation, your relationships, your sleep, your sense of right and wrong. Some jobs pay well. Some jobs haunt you. Most do both.

You will remember the faces behind the objectives. The voices on the comms. The moment things went wrong. The moment you chose not to stop.

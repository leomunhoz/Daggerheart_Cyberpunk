THE WORLD BEFORE YOU

For decades, corporations grew beyond governments. Nations became dependent on corporate technology, infrastructure, and private military forces. Resources became scarce and profit became the closest thing humanity had to a common language.

The Corporate Wars reshaped the world. Entire cities were erased. Economies collapsed. Nations fragmented.

THE CRASH

Four years ago, during the Third Corporate War, an experimental military AI known as Hades was unleashed by a legendary mercenary under circumstances that remain unclear.

The AI spread through the global network faster than anyone believed possible. In a few hours, it corrupted military systems, civilian infrastructure, satellites, and eventually other AIs.

Humanity won only by killing the internet as a whole. Entire archives of human knowledge disappeared overnight and the modern world collapsed alongside it.

THE BLACK WALL

To contain what survived the Crash, the corporations built the Black Wall, a digital quarantine for the corrupted AI. Beyond it remain rogue AIs, corrupted systems, and fragments of technologies humanity no longer fully understands.

Although the Black Wall contains a large portion of the AIs, some remain hosted on previously compromised devices. In some cases, they may lie dormant for months or years, but eventually "wake up" and corrupt nearby devices, creating Haunted Zones and manifesting their Digital Ghosts.

THE EIDOLONS

Some technologies survived the Crash. Others returned from it.

The Eidolons are among the most valuable military assets ever created: massive combat platforms capable of turning a single pilot into an army. No one fully understands how they work.

Every Eidolon requires an artificial intelligence sophisticated enough to share the burden of piloting with a human mind, most using either Ares or Athena AI. Every pilot pays the price in sanity for that connection.

AI GODS

Most corporate systems descend from corrupted AI known as "The Theoi": ancient Deus-Class AI from which countless lesser AIs, daemons, ghosts, and corporate systems descend.

Weather systems, medical prediction, logistics, communications, fabrication, surveillance, finance, and war all depended on central intelligences too vast for ordinary networks to understand. Their creators named them after gods. After the Collapse, those names became less like codenames and more like warnings.

Every major corporation claims to use only clean, legal, post-Collapse AI — but that’s a lie.

THE CORPS

Some of the Major Corporations are:

  • Ava Biodyne: Medical empire, synthetic organs, trauma insurance. Ava sells survival with a payment plan.

  • Illiat Cognition: Identity modeling, predictive behavior, memory editing. Illiat does not ask who you are. It tells you.

  • Tyfar Armaments: Urban pacification, personal defense, tactical gear. Tyfar believes every problem has an acceptable caliber.

CHARACTER ADVANCEMENT AND CYBERWARE

In Edgeheart, characters still advance in level, gain access to stronger Competency cards, improve their class and subclass features, and unlock higher-Tier options as normal.

However, level and Tier advancement no longer grant the advancement options for Hit Points, Stress, Evasion, Damage Thresholds, or Character Traits. Those improvements come primarily from Cyberware.

Cyberware is how characters become faster, tougher, sharper, stronger, harder to kill, or more deeply connected to the systems of the New Dark Age. Installing new chrome can increase your body’s limits, improve your combat performance, expand your senses, enhance your mind, or give you access to abilities no ordinary human could survive.

This means character growth in Edgeheart is not only about training or experience. It is also about what you choose to replace, reinforce, upgrade, or sacrifice.

THE WORLD TODAY

Most cities are now controlled by gangs acting as governments, corps acting as nations, and mercenaries acting as soldiers. War has simply become another market. Peace exists only where someone can afford to buy it.

WHAT YOU ARE

You are a mercenary willing to do what others cannot. Some work for corporations, others fight for gangs, or to protect the remnants of nations. Most simply chase the next paycheck. You may become famous, feared, or hunted, but every job carries a price.

WHAT YOU NEED TO KNOW

  • Corporations are the true owners of the world.

  • The global Internet is dead.

  • The Black Wall separates humanity from rogue AIs, but it is also a territory teeming with relics and profit.

  • Cyberware is power, and power always comes with a price, whether financial, physical, or mental.

  • Eidolons are rare, valuable, and capable of changing wars.

  • Reputation is currency, and legends rarely die peacefully.

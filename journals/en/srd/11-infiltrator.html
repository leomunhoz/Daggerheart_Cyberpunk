Other mercenaries can hide, sneak, or bypass security. Infiltrators turn those moments into a profession. They feel the exact second when a target becomes vulnerable.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-classes.Item.{{id:infiltrator:class}}]{Infiltrator}

CLASS DETAILS

  • Competency: Ghost

  • Starting Evasion: 12

  • Starting Hit Points: 5

  • Class Items: A set of false credentials or a suppressed weapon from a job that officially never happened.

HOPE FEATURE

Clean Exit: Spend 3 Hope when you would be discovered, trigger an alarm, or be targeted by an attack. You immediately become Hidden and move to a point within Close range.

  • If this was triggered by an attack, the attack has disadvantage.
  • If this was triggered by discovery or an alarm, it is delayed until your next Spotlight.

CLASS FEATURES

Cover Work: You have a resource called Cover. When you roll with Hope while using a Ghost card, using stealth, disguise, misdirection, or a prepared route, gain 1 Cover. You can hold up to 3 Cover.

Spend 1 Cover to do one of the following:

  • Setup: Gain a +2 bonus to a roll made to hide, infiltrate, sabotage, impersonate someone, or bypass security.
  • Takedown: Add 1d6 to the damage roll when you deal damage to a target you are Hidden from, that is unaware of you, or that is Vulnerable.

You lose all unspent Cover when the scene ends.

SUBCLASSES

Choose between Silent Killer or Phantom. (The PDF's list calls the second one "Deep Cover", but its section is named Phantom.)

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:infiltrator:Silent Killer:subclass}}]{Silent Killer}

Play as a Silent Killer if you want to eliminate targets with precision and end fights before they begin.

Interface Trait: Finesse

Foundation Feature

  • Opening Strike: Once per Spotlight, when you deal damage to a target while you are Hidden from them or while they are Vulnerable, add a number of d6s equal to your Tier to the damage roll.

  • No Witnesses: When you defeat or disable a target with an attack you made while Hidden, you can immediately become Hidden again and move to a point within Very Close range.

Specialization Feature

  • Kill Window: When you spend Cover on Takedown, you roll a d8 instead of a d6. Additionally, if the target is Vulnerable, they must also mark a Stress.

Mastery Feature

  • Perfect Ambush: Once per long rest, when you make a successful attack against a target you are Hidden from, do not roll your weapon damage dice. Instead, use the highest possible value of those dice.

    If the target survives the attack, they become permanently Vulnerable.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:infiltrator:Phantom:subclass}}]{Phantom}

Play as a Phantom if you want to slip through guarded spaces, reposition through blind spots, extract allies from danger, and disappear before the enemy can lock you down.

Interface Trait: Agility

Foundation Feature

  • Ghost Route: When you move while Hidden, you can move within Far range instead of Close range.

    If you start this movement within Very Close range of an ally, you can bring them with you. That ally doesn’t become Hidden unless they were already Hidden.

  • Now You See Me: Once per scene, when an attack against you misses or has disadvantage, you can gain 1 Cover.

Specialization Feature

  • Extraction Window: When an ally within Close range would be targeted by an attack, discovered, or caught by a danger, you can spend 1 Cover to make that ally immediately move to a point within Close range.

    The triggering attack has disadvantage against them, or the ally has advantage on the roll to avoid it.

Mastery Feature

  • Impossible Exit: Once per rest, you can spend 3 Cover to reveal an escape route you prepared or noticed. You and any allies within Close range can immediately move within Far range, ignoring difficult terrain, simple alarms, and zones controlled by enemies during this movement.

    After this movement, you become Hidden. Any ally who ends this movement in cover, darkness, smoke, a crowd, or another plausible hiding place also becomes Hidden.

ORIGIN QUESTIONS

Answer any of the following origin questions. You can also create your own questions.

  • What was the first place you broke into that should have been impossible?

  • Did you become an Infiltrator through training, coercion, desperation, or betrayal?

  • What target did you fail to eliminate, and what did it cost you?

CONNECTIONS

Ask one of the following questions to your fellow players for their characters to answer, or create your own questions.

  • Which character knows one of your false identities?

  • Who do you trust to cover your exit?

  • What secret did you learn about another character but choose not to use?

DILEMMAS

Consider the following dilemmas of Humanity and Cyberpsychosis.

  • Humanity: You get people out, avoid collateral damage, and remember that not every problem needs a body count.

  • Cyberpsychosis: You begin to treat identities as disposable, people as obstacles, and killing as the cleanest solution.

Other mercenaries can use Network tools, but Runners live inside the breach. They are intrusion specialists, signal thieves, and digital exploiters who survive by turning every system into an opening.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-classes.Item.{{id:runner:class}}]{Runner}

CLASS DETAILS

  • Competency: Network

  • Starting Evasion: 10

  • Starting Hit Points: 5

  • Class Items: An illegal cyberdeck with a custom interface or a stolen access shard from a job that should have killed you.

HOPE FEATURE

Zero-Day: Spend 3 Hope to immediately create 2 Breaches.

CLASS FEATURES

Hold Your Breach: After you create a Breach, spend a Hope to use it without spending it.

Exploit: Mark a Stress to temporarily Exploit a target within Far range. While Exploited, the target gains a penalty to their Thresholds and Difficulty equal to your Tier. The maximum number of creatures you can Exploit at one time is equal to your Interface trait.

Hacking and Breaches

When you succeed on an Interface Roll to hack a connected system, you create a Breach. A Breach is a temporary opening in a system. When you create a Breach, spend it immediately to choose one Hack: Open/Lock, Loop/Spoof, Pull Data, Jam, Expose, or Command.

SUBCLASSES

Choose between Breach Specialist or Net Diver. (The PDF's list calls the second one "Signal Ghost", but its section is named Net Diver.)

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:runner:Breach Specialist:subclass}}]{Breach Specialist}

Play as a Breach Specialist if you want to weaponize intrusion, break enemy systems, crash defenses, and turn networks into attack vectors.

Interface Trait: Knowledge

Foundation Feature

  • Exploit Payload: When you Exploit a creature, drone, vehicle, or connected system, you can mark a Stress to make the target temporarily Vulnerable.

  • Hard Crash: Once per rest, when you create a Breach against a target you have Exploited, you can immediately use that Breach without spending it.

Specialization Feature

  • Cascade Breach: Once per scene, when you create a Breach, you can immediately create another Breach on a connected target or system within Close range of the original target.

Mastery Feature

  • Rootkill: Once per long rest, when you successfully Hack a target you have Exploited, you can force a catastrophic system failure. The target marks 1 Stress and 1 Hit Point, then becomes temporarily Degraded. While Degraded, the target uses a d12 instead of a d20 for attack rolls.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:runner:Net Diver:subclass}}]{Net Diver}

Play as a Net Diver if you want to enter hostile networks, control connected systems, and complete the job from inside the digital architecture.

Interface Trait: Instinct

Foundation Feature

  • Deep Dive: Once per scene, when you create a Breach in a network, you can enter a Deep Dive. While in a Deep Dive: you can interact with connected systems in the scene regardless of range; you gain a bonus equal to your Tier on Interface Rolls involving Hacking or Network cards; you can ask the GM one question about the network, its security, connected systems, or hidden routes. The Deep Dive ends when you take Major damage, disconnect, or the scene ends.

Specialization Feature

  • Virtual Overwatch: When an adversary targets you or an ally within Very Close range with an attack while you are in a Deep Dive, you can give them disadvantage on the roll. If the adversary fails the roll, they must also mark a Stress. If they succeed, you must mark a Stress.

Mastery Feature

  • Blackout Cascade: Once per long rest, while in a Deep Dive, you can trigger a massive network disruption. Choose up to three connected systems, devices, or cyberware-linked targets in the scene. For each one, choose one: Disable it until the GM spends a Fear; Feed it false information, giving disadvantage on its next roll; Overload it, causing its operator to mark 1 Stress; Open, lock, jam, redirect, control, or trigger it in a way that significantly changes the scene. Afterward, mark a Stress or become temporarily Vulnerable.

ORIGIN QUESTIONS

Answer any of the following origin questions. You can also create your own questions.

  • Who taught you how to run systems, and what did they warn you never to touch?

  • What was the first network you broke into, and what did you find there?

  • Which corporation, gang, or government system still has your trace buried somewhere inside it?

CONNECTIONS

Ask one of the following questions to your fellow players for their characters to answer, or create your own questions.

  • I once erased evidence that could have ruined you. What did I delete?

  • You saw me jack into a system that nearly killed me. What did you do to pull me out?

  • I know a secret about you because of something I found in a network. Do you know that I know?

DILEMMAS

Consider the following dilemmas of Humanity and Cyberpsychosis.

  • Humanity: You use your skills to protect people from surveillance, corporate control, and digital erasure.

  • Cyberpsychosis: You begin to see people as systems, secrets as leverage, and every closed door as something that exists only to be opened.

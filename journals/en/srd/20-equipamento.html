CHAPTER TWO: CHOOSE YOUR EQUIPMENT

Players begin with one Tier 1 Primary Weapon and one Tier 1 Armor. If their Primary Weapon is One-Handed, they may also begin with one Tier 1 Secondary Weapon.

Normal equipment does not increase Cyber Load. Cyberware, Redline implants, Blackwall relics, and Eidolons are handled separately.

ECONOMY OF THE NEW DARK AGE

In Edgeheart, money is measured in Credits, usually called Creds by mercenaries, fixers, gangs, and street dealers. Use broad amounts to represent spending power.

The usual exchange is:

10 Stacks = 1 Case  |  10 Cases = 1 Vault

  • 1 Stack: A small amount of credits, enough for food, bribes, cheap tools, basic supplies, or street expenses.

  • 1 Case: A serious payment, enough for equipment, vehicle repairs, cyberware installation, or meaningful favors.

  • 1 Vault: A small fortune, enough for rare cyberware, elite equipment, Eidolon parts, or life-changing contracts.

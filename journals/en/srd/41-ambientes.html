Environments represent everything in a scene beyond the PCs and adversaries, such as megabuildings, streets, black clinics, highways, corporate towers, digital hauntings, Eidolon battlefields, and the pressure of the New Dark Age itself. As needed, reflavor some adversaries, as instructed in each Environment sheet.

Environment features are Passive, Action, or Reaction; the italic question after each one helps connect it to the fiction.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-environments.Actor.{{id:environment:Highway Kill Run}}]{Highway Kill Run}

A high-speed chase through broken roads, traffic drones, abandoned checkpoints, and ambush vehicles.

Impulses: Accelerate, split vehicles, weaponize traffic
Difficulty: 14 | Potential Adversaries: Corporate Response Team, Contract Sniper, Blood Saint Duelist, Cordon Eidolon

  • The Chase — Passive (d8): When this Traversal starts, place a Chase Die (d8) on this card with the 4 value facing up. When a PC makes an action related to the chase, turn the die according to the result. When a PC succeeds with Hope, increase the die by 2. When a PC succeeds with Fear, increase the die by 1. When a PC fails with Hope, decrease the die by 1. When a PC fails with Fear, decrease the die by 2. If the die reach the 8 value, the PC succeed in the Highway Kill Run, either by scaping or for having reached the target. If the die reach 0, the PC failed, either getting captured or letting the target escape. Why are we chasing them, anyway?

  • Vehicle Momentum — Passive: When a PC succeeds on a vehicle roll with Hope, they may move one additional range or create an opening for an ally. Who knows this road best?

  • Road Hazard — Action: Drop traffic, wreckage, drones, civilians, or a collapsing overpass into the route. Drivers must make an Agility Reaction Roll or the vehicle becomes Damaged. What did the road hide?

  • Pursuit Escalation — Reaction: When a PC rolls with Fear while driving, a new hostile vehicle enters at Far range. Who joined the chase?

The GM can roll on these tables, choose an item, or use them as inspiration to create unique rewards. Loot can be kept and used repeatedly until lost, destroyed, traded, or removed by the fiction. Consumables are used once, then removed from your inventory.

  • Common (1d10): Frequently recovered from supply lockers, abandoned apartments, street markets, storage crates, roadside caches, or standard contract rewards.

  • Uncommon (1d10 or 2d10): Found in secured rooms, gang armories, hidden caches, black-market drops, restricted clinics, or carried by elite enemies.

  • Rare (2d10 or 3d10): Recovered from corporate facilities, blacksites, military transports, dangerous dead zones, powerful commanders, or high-risk contract objectives.

  • Legendary (3d10 or 4d10): Unique prototypes, lost-tech relics, Blackwall artifacts, experimental hardware, Eidolon-linked components, or treasures claimed from the most dangerous jobs in the New Dark Age.

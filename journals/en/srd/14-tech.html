Techs are problem solvers who understand that every machine has a way to break, bend, or become useful. They make impossible plans work just long enough to matter.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-classes.Item.{{id:tech:class}}]{Tech}

CLASS DETAILS

  • Competency: Systems

  • Starting Evasion: 10

  • Starting Hit Points: 5

  • Class Items: A customized toolkit full of illegal adapters and scavenged parts, or a broken machine you refuse to stop repairing.

HOPE FEATURE

Make It Work: Spend 3 Hope after you or an ally within Close range fails an action roll using a device, weapon, or Systems card. Describe your emergency fix, then the target can reroll their Duality Dice and use the new result.

CLASS FEATURES

Jury-Rig: Once per scene, when you have a few moments and access to your tools, you can modify, repair, or improvise with a device, weapon, or piece of machinery within Melee range. Choose one:

  • Boost: The next time the modified item is used before the scene ends, add +1d6 to its action roll, Interface Roll, or damage roll.
  • Patch: Restore a broken, jammed, disabled, or damaged item so it functions until the scene ends, or allow a creature using the item to clear an Armor Slot.
  • Rewire: Connect or disconnect the item from a network, bypass its local controls, or allow it to be operated from Far range until the scene ends.

You can mark a Stress to use Jury-Rig again during the same scene.

SUBCLASSES

Choose between Rigger or Saboteur.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:tech:Rigger:subclass}}]{Rigger}

Play as a Rigger if you want to control drones, operate machines remotely, and support allies through equipment.

Interface Trait: Knowledge

Foundation Feature

  • Companion Drone: You have a small drone or machine companion that can move independently within Far range, carry small objects, record, scan, and interact with controls.

    You may Help an ally within Close range of the drone regardless of your own position. If their roll involves equipment or connected systems, use a d8 as your Help die.

    When the drone would take damage, mark a Stress to move it out of danger. Otherwise, it is disabled until your next rest.

Specialization Feature

  • Remote Hands: You may use Jury-Rig through your drone as though you were touching the target. After using Boost or Patch, the drone may immediately move within Close range.

Mastery Feature

  • Drone Network: Once per long rest, deploy a drone network until the scene ends or you take Severe damage. While active:

    • Your drone can operate within Very Far range.
    • Jury-Rig used through it may apply two effects.
    • When an ally within Close range of it takes damage, mark a Stress to reduce that damage by 1d8.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:tech:Saboteur:subclass}}]{Saboteur}

Play as a Saboteur if you want to plant charges, disable defenses, and turn the environment against your enemies.

Interface Trait: Finesse

Foundation Feature

  • Planted Charge: When you can touch an object, surface, or machine, mark a Stress to plant a charge. Trigger it later in the scene to choose one:

    • Breach: Open, break, or collapse the target.
    • Disable: Jam, lock, or shut it down until repaired.
    • Disrupt: One adversary within Very Close range marks 1 Stress and becomes temporarily Vulnerable.

    You can have one active charge at a time.

Specialization Feature

  • Cascading Failure: You can have two active charges. When one is triggered:

    • Breach: You or an ally Close to it may immediately move within Close range.
    • Disable: An adversary using the target also marks 1 Stress.
    • Disrupt: The affected adversary has disadvantage on their next action roll before your next Spotlight.

Mastery Feature

  • Controlled Demolition: Once per long rest, when you trigger a charge, destroy, collapse, open, or disable its target to create a major breach, escape route, or obstruction.

    Adversaries within Close range make a Reaction Roll (16). On a failure, they take 3d10 physical damage and become temporarily Restrained. On a success, they take half damage.

ORIGIN QUESTIONS

Answer any of the following origin questions. You can also create your own questions.

  • What machine, weapon, drone, or device do you refuse to abandon?

  • What piece of technology failed you when you needed it most?

  • Did you become a Tech because you loved machines, or because people were less reliable?

CONNECTIONS

Ask one of the following questions to your fellow players for their characters to answer, or create your own questions.

  • Who broke something important that you still have not forgiven?

  • Who trusts your machines more than they trust you?

  • Which character helped you carry, hide, or rebuild something illegal?

DILEMMAS

Consider the following dilemmas of Humanity and Cyberpsychosis.

  • Humanity: You build things to keep people alive, create options, and give the crew a way out.

  • Cyberpsychosis: You begin to see people as faulty machines, bodies as inefficient hardware, and failure as something to be corrected at any cost.

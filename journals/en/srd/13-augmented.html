Augmented are mercenaries who turned their own bodies into their livelihood. They know how to move with metal bones, strike with calibrated limbs, survive with artificial organs, and push their systems without immediately burning out.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-classes.Item.{{id:augmented:class}}]{Augmented}

CLASS DETAILS

  • Competency: Chrome

  • Starting Evasion: 11

  • Starting Hit Points: 6

  • Class Items: A maintenance kit for your most important implant or the original piece of your body that was replaced by chrome.

HOPE FEATURE

Chrome Surge: Spend 3 Hope when you make an Agility, Strength, or Finesse Roll. Roll a d8 and add its result to the roll. If this roll succeeds, choose one:

  • Immediately move within Close range.
  • Clear a Stress.
  • Gain a +2 bonus to your Evasion until your next Spotlight.

CLASS FEATURES

Integrated Chrome: Your cyberware is fully integrated into your nervous system. When you finish a rest, choose Agility, Strength, or Finesse as your Calibrated Trait.

When you make an action roll using your Calibrated Trait or use a Chrome card, you can mark a Stress to roll a d6 and add its result to the roll.

Additionally, when you roll your Humanity Die to resist Cyberpsychosis, treat your Cyber Load as 1 lower, to a minimum of 0.

SUBCLASSES

Choose between Reflex Suite or Titan Frame.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:augmented:Reflex Suite:subclass}}]{Reflex Suite}

Play as a Reflex Suite if you want to move faster than human reaction time and turn your nervous system into a weapon.

Interface Trait: Agility

Foundation Feature

  • Neural Reflexes: When you use Integrated Chrome on an Agility or Finesse Roll, you can immediately move within Very Close range after the roll resolves. If that roll succeeds with Hope, you can move within Close range instead.

  • Fast Enough: Once per rest, when an attack against you would hit, you can mark a Stress to roll a d6 and add its result to your Evasion against that attack.

Specialization Feature

  • Motion Blur: When you move within Close range or farther during your Spotlight, the next attack made against you before your next Spotlight has disadvantage.

Mastery Feature

  • Accelerated Loop: Once per long rest, you can push your reflexware into full acceleration. Until the scene ends or you take Severe damage, the first time each Spotlight that you use Integrated Chrome on a successful Agility or Finesse Roll, you can clear a Stress or immediately move within Very Close range.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:augmented:Titan Frame:subclass}}]{Titan Frame}

Play as a Titan Frame if you want to become a mountain of heavy chrome and industrial strength that refuses to move unless you allow it.

Interface Trait: Strength

Foundation Feature

  • Reinforced Build: When your Calibrated Trait is Strength, gain a +1 bonus to your Armor Score. Additionally, when you use Integrated Chrome on a Strength Roll, roll a d8 instead of a d6.

  • Load-Bearing Body: You have advantage on rolls to resist being pushed, knocked down, disarmed, moved against your will, or temporarily Restrained.

Specialization Feature

  • Impact Absorbers: Once per scene, when you would mark one or more Hit Points from physical or impact damage, you can mark a Stress to reduce the severity by one threshold.

    If this prevents you from marking any Hit Points, you can immediately move within Very Close range or force the attacker to mark 1 Stress.

Mastery Feature

  • Heavy Chrome Walking: Once per long rest, when you mark an Armor Slot, you can activate your full-body reinforcement until the scene ends or you take Severe damage. While this feature is active:

    • When you make a successful Melee attack roll, you can add your Calibrated Trait die to the damage roll.
    • Gain a +3 bonus to your damage thresholds.
    • When you succeed on a Strength Roll or weapon attack against a target within Melee range, you can push them back to Close range and make them temporarily Vulnerable.

ORIGIN QUESTIONS

Answer any of the following origin questions. You can also create your own questions.

  • What was your first implant, and why did you get it?

  • Which part of your original body do you miss most?

  • Did you choose to become an Augmented, or was your body rebuilt after something terrible?

CONNECTIONS

Ask one of the following questions to your fellow players for their characters to answer, or create your own questions.

  • Which character knew you before the chrome?

  • Who helped you survive a bad installation?

  • Who treats your body like a person, not a machine?

DILEMMAS

Consider the following dilemmas of Humanity and Cyberpsychosis.

  • Humanity: Your chrome helps you protect, endure, and keep moving without forgetting the body is still yours.

  • Cyberpsychosis: You begin to see flesh as weakness, pain as bad engineering, and identity as something that can be replaced part by part.

TIER 1 — STREET LEVEL THREATS

The PDF only gives the Neon Claw Ganger's type (Minion). In the module, it follows the official Minion pattern — Minion (3) and Group Attack, like the Jagged Knife Lackey — and the others received the type that fits their role: Sitil Security Guard (Standard), Chrome Maw Bruiser (Bruiser), Tiger Choir Cutter (Skulk), Pocket Drone Handler (Support), and Rookie Edgerunner (Ranged).

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Neon Claw Ganger}}]{Neon Claw Ganger}

A loud ganger with cheap chrome, bright colors, and something to prove.

Motives & Tactics: Swarm, posture, protect turf
Difficulty: 10 | Thresholds: None | HP: 1 | Stress: 1
ATK: −2 | Street Blade: Melee | 3 phy
Experience: Street Violence +1

  • Minion (3) — Passive: This adversary is defeated when they take any damage. For every 3 damage a PC deals to them, defeat an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

  • Group Attack — Action: Spend a Fear to choose a target and spotlight all Neon Claw Gangers within Close range of them. Those Minions move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 3 physical damage each. Combine this damage.

  • Show of Force — Reaction: When another gang ally within Close range deals damage, this Ganger may immediately move within Very Close range of the damaged target.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Sitil Security Guard}}]{Sitil Security Guard}

A private security guard trained to delay intruders until the real deal arrives.

Motives & Tactics: Delay, call backup, protect property
Difficulty: 11 | Thresholds: 6/12 | HP: 4 | Stress: 2
ATK: +1 | Security Rifle: Far | 1d8+2 phy
Experience: Corporate Procedure +2

  • Badge Discipline — Passive: While within Close range of a corporate device, checkpoint, camera, or locked door, this adversary gains +1 Difficulty.

  • Call It In — Action: Mark a Stress to activate local security. The next corporate adversary spotlighted gains advantage on their action roll.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Chrome Maw Bruiser}}]{Chrome Maw Bruiser}

A gang enforcer with reinforced jaws, hydraulic arms, and a body built for intimidation.

Motives & Tactics: Break bones, threaten, hold ground
Difficulty: 12 | Thresholds: 9/16 | HP: 5 | Stress: 3
ATK: +2 | Chrome Fists: Melee | 2d6+2 impact
Experience: Intimidating Chrome +2

  • Chrome Bulk — Passive: The first time this adversary would mark HP, they may mark a Stress instead.

  • Jawbreaker — Action: Mark a Stress to attack a target within Melee range. On a success, the target must mark a Stress or become temporarily Vulnerable.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Tiger Choir Cutter}}]{Tiger Choir Cutter}

A masked blade who moves through crowds like a rumor with a knife.

Motives & Tactics: Ambush, isolate, cut reputation
Difficulty: 12 | Thresholds: 5/10 | HP: 4 | Stress: 3
ATK: +2 | Mono-Knife: Melee | 1d8+2 phy
Experience: Street Ambush +2

  • Crowd Slip — Passive: When spotlighted in a crowd, club, market, alley, or chaotic street, this adversary becomes Hidden until it acts.

  • Reputation Cut — Action: Mark a Stress to attack a target within Melee range. On a success, deal damage and the target cannot spent Hope until the Cutter next Spotlight.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Pocket Drone Handler}}]{Pocket Drone Handler}

A nervous operator surrounded by cheap combat drones and too many screens.

Motives & Tactics: Mark targets, hide behind machines, call signals
Difficulty: 11 | Thresholds: 5/10 | HP: 3 | Stress: 3
ATK: +0 | Shock Drone: Far | 1d6+2 techno
Experience: Drone Operations +2

  • Spotter Swarm — Passive: The first corporate or gang ally to attack a target damaged by this adversary gains +1 to the attack roll.

  • Send the Drone — Action: Mark a Stress to move a drone anywhere within Far range. A target within Very Close range of the drone must make an Agility Reaction Roll or take the 1d6+2 techno damage and mark a Stress.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Rookie Edgerunner}}]{Rookie Edgerunner}

A young mercenary with rented gear, real ambition, and bad judgment.

Motives & Tactics: Take cover, show off, survive the job
Difficulty: 12 | Thresholds: 5/11 | HP: 4 | Stress: 2
ATK: +2 | Smart Pistol: Far | 1d8+1 phy
Experience: First Contract +2

  • Cover Shooter — Passive: While behind cover, this adversary gains +1 Difficulty.

  • Panic Burst — Reaction: When this adversary marks HP, mark a Stress to immediately make a standard attack with disadvantage.

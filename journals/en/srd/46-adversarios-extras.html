EXTRA ADVERSARIES

These adversaries are not in the Edgeheart PDF. They were made for the module to round out the factions the way Daggerheart does: each one uses the numbers and features of an official adversary with the same role, keeping the system's automation, with the text reframed for the setting. The official template appears next to each name.

BUILDING A FIGHT

Daggerheart builds encounters with a Battle Point budget: 3 × the number of PCs + 2. Each adversary costs points by role:

CostRole
1Minions (a group the size of the table), Social, Support
2Horde, Ranged, Skulk, Standard
3Leader
4Bruiser
5Solo

With the adversaries below, the Police, the Chrome Maw, and Ava Biodyne can each carry a single-faction fight, mixing cheap and expensive pieces.

CITY POLICE AND PSYCHO SQUAD

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:City Patrol Officer}}]{City Patrol Officer}

Tier 1 · Minion · template: Sellsword. An underpaid patrol cop with a ballistic vest, a radio on the shoulder, and little desire to die for the salary.

Motives & Tactics: surround, call for backup, enforce order
Difficulty: 10 | Thresholds: none | HP: 1 | Stress: 1
ATK: +3 | Service Pistol: Close | 3 phy

  • Minion (4) — Passive: defeated when they take any damage. For every 4 damage a PC deals to them, defeat an additional Minion within range the attack would succeed against.

  • Group Attack — Action: spend a Fear to spotlight all Patrol Officers within Close range of a target. They move into Melee range and make one shared attack roll; each deals 3 physical damage, combined.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Patrol Sergeant}}]{Patrol Sergeant}

Tier 1 · Leader · template: Head Guard. A veteran sergeant who has seen too many gangs, corps, and cyberpsychos, and still comes back for the night shift.

Motives & Tactics: coordinate the patrol, contain the scene, bring someone in cuffed
Difficulty: 15 | Thresholds: 7/13 | HP: 7 | Stress: 3
ATK: +4 | Stun Baton: Melee | 1d10+4 phy
Experiences: Chain of Command +2, Beat Knowledge +2

  • Rally the Squad — Action: spend 2 Fear to spotlight the Sergeant and up to 2d4 allies within Far range.

  • On My Signal — Reaction: Countdown (5). Activate it the first time the Sergeant is spotlighted; it ticks down when a PC makes an attack roll. When it triggers, all Patrol Officers within Far range attack the nearest target with advantage; combine damage on the same target.

  • Momentum — Reaction: when the Sergeant hits a PC, you gain a Fear.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Riot Squad}}]{Riot Squad}

Tier 2 · Horde · template: Archer Squadron. A line of riot cops with ballistic shields, gas launchers, and shock batons, advancing like a wall.

Motives & Tactics: form the line, disperse the crowd, push until it breaks
Difficulty: 13 | Thresholds: 8/16 | HP: 4 | Stress: 3
ATK: +0 | Riot Guns: Far | 2d6+3 phy

  • Gas Volley — Action: spend a Fear to target a point within Far range and attack everyone within Close range of it with advantage; targets hit take 1d10+4 physical damage.

  • Suppressing Fire — Action: mark a Stress to target a point within Far range. Until the next roll with Fear, anyone who moves within Close range of it makes an Agility Reaction Roll: 2d6+3 physical damage on a failure, half on a success.

  • Horde (1d6+3) — Passive: with half or more HP marked, the standard attack deals 1d6+3 physical damage.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Riot Lieutenant}}]{Riot Lieutenant}

Tier 3 · Solo · template: Fire Titan. A riot lieutenant who is more chrome than flesh, holding an entire street alone behind a bullet-scarred shield.

Motives & Tactics: advance slowly, break the barricade, never give ground
Difficulty: 18 | Thresholds: 19/35 | HP: 10 | Stress: 5
ATK: +3 | Shock Shotgun: Close | 3d12+4 phy
Experiences: Riot Control +5, Tactics +3

  • Relentless (3) — Passive: can be spotlighted up to three times per GM turn.

  • Riot Plating — Passive: Countdown (3), active when they appear; it ticks down when they take Major or Severe damage. Until it triggers, reduce incoming damage by 1d12. When it triggers, they gain +1 to Difficulty and attack rolls.

  • Momentum — Passive: when they hit a PC, you gain a Fear.

  • Shield Charge — Action: mark a Stress to charge in a straight line to a point within Far range and attack everyone in the path; targets hit take 3d12 physical damage.

  • Point-Blank Blast — Action: mark a Stress to place a token on the stat block. On the next spotlight, spend it and attack a PC within Close range: 3d20+5 direct physical damage on a success; the target marks a Stress on a failure.

  • Combat Stims — Reaction: when they mark half or more of their HP, spend a Fear and mark any number of Stress to clear that many HP.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Psycho Squad Commander}}]{Psycho Squad Commander}

Tier 4 · Solo · template: Fallen Warlord: Undefeated Champion. The commander of the anti-cyberpsycho unit: mantis blades in both forearms, reflexes no one should have, and the conviction that it takes a monster to hunt one.

Motives & Tactics: hunt, isolate the prey, execute in public
Difficulty: 18 | Thresholds: 35/58 | HP: 11 | Stress: 5
ATK: +8 | Mantis Blades: Very Close | 4d12+13 phy
Experiences: Manhunt +3, Case Files +2, Intimidation +3

  • Relentless (3) — Passive: can be spotlighted up to three times per GM turn.

  • Carbon Plating — Passive: when she takes damage, reduce it by 1d10.

  • Mantis Sweep — Action: mark a Stress to attack everyone within Very Close range in a spin of the blades; PCs hit lose Hope equal to the HP they marked.

  • Call the Squad — Action: spend a Fear to call two Psycho Squad Operators, who appear at Far range.

  • Containment Zone — Reaction: Countdown (1d8), active on the first spotlight. When it triggers, a containment zone covers an area within Far range; anyone inside is Vulnerable until they leave. It drops if she takes Severe damage.

  • Momentum — Reaction: when she hits a PC, you gain a Fear.

  • Public Execution — Reaction: when a target marks HP from her attack, all PCs within Far range of the target lose a Hope.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Theoi Avatar}}]{Theoi Avatar}

Tier 4 · Solo · template: Oracle of Doom. The projection of one of the Theoi AIs: a mass of digital noise with a cracked porcelain mask, which has already calculated your future before you walked into the room.

Motives & Tactics: predict, decree, rewrite the fate of mortals
Difficulty: 20 | Thresholds: 38/68 | HP: 11 | Stress: 10
ATK: +8 | Probability Collapse: Far | 4d8+9 techno
Experience: Predictive Omniscience +4

  • Terrifying — Passive: when it hits, all PCs within Far range lose a Hope and you gain a Fear.

  • System Closing In — Passive: anyone who rolls a failure within Very Far range of it marks a Stress.

  • Pronounce Fate — Action: spend a Fear to show a target within Far range a simulation of their nightmare. Knowledge Reaction Roll: on a failure, they lose all Hope and take 2d20+4 direct techno damage; on a success, half and they lose a Hope.

  • Summon Daemons — Action: once per day, spend 2 Fear to summon 2d4 Tier 2 or lower Minions tied to a PC's nightmare, within Close range of them.

  • Total Prediction — Reaction: when it sees a mortal creature, it knows one of their personal nightmares.

  • Countermeasure — Reaction: when it marks HP from an attack within Very Close range, mark a Stress to knock the attacker back to Far range and deal 2d10+4 physical damage.

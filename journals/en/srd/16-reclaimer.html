Reclaimers are survivors who know how to live where civilization failed. They cross dead cities and see routes, shelter, and resources of the old world waiting to be used.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-classes.Item.{{id:reclaimer:class}}]{Reclaimer}

CLASS DETAILS

  • Competency: Frontier

  • Starting Evasion: 11

  • Starting Hit Points: 6

  • Class Items: A cracked route-map filled with personal markings or a piece of salvage from the place that should have killed you.

HOPE FEATURE

Home Ground: Spend 3 Hope when an adversary within Far range moves, attacks, or targets you or an ally to use the terrain against them. They must make a Reaction Roll (14).

On a failure, they become temporarily Vulnerable or temporarily Restrained and mark 1 Stress. If they are operating a vehicle, you may instead make that vehicle become Damaged. On a success, they mark 1 Stress.

CLASS FEATURES

Surveyed Route: Spend a Hope and take a moment to read the terrain, ruins, or streets around you. Make an Instinct Roll (12). On a success, the area within Far range becomes your Surveyed Route until the scene ends or you take Severe damage. While you are within your Surveyed Route:

  • You and allies within Close range can ignore difficult terrain while moving or driving.
  • When you deal damage to an adversary in the zone, you can force them to mark 1 Stress.
  • When you fail an action roll, you can end your Surveyed Route to reroll your Duality Dice.

SUBCLASSES

Choose between Outrider or Zonebreaker.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:reclaimer:Outrider:subclass}}]{Outrider}

Play as an Outrider if you want to master vehicles, lead pursuits, and survive impossible roads.

Interface Trait: Agility

Foundation Feature

  • Signature Vehicle: You have a personal vehicle. When operating it, you may use Agility instead of its listed Trait.

    Once per scene, prevent your Signature Vehicle from becoming Damaged or from becoming Disabled. You may designate a new Signature Vehicle during a rest.

Specialization Feature

  • Stunt Driver: Once per scene while operating your Signature Vehicle, describe a stunt and make an Agility Roll (15). On a success, choose two. On a failure, choose one, then your vehicle becomes Damaged or you mark 1 Stress.

    • Move within Far range.
    • Give one ally +2 to their next action roll.
    • Make one adversary mark 1 Stress.

Mastery Feature

  • Drive Off: Once per long rest, when you succeed on a driving roll with your Signature Vehicle, you may move within Far range or clear 1 Stress.

    Additionally, one adversary makes a Reaction Roll (16). On a failure, it takes 3d10 physical damage and becomes temporarily Vulnerable. On a success, it takes half damage.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:reclaimer:Zonebreaker:subclass}}]{Zonebreaker}

Play as a Zonebreaker if you want to dominate terrain, turn ruins into weapons, and make the battlefield itself dangerous.

Interface Trait: Instinct

Foundation Feature

  • Field Salvage: Once per scene, you can scavenge useful material from the surrounding area. Choose one piece of Field Salvage:

    • Patch: You or an ally within Melee range clears an Armor Slot.
    • Tool: You or an ally gains a +2 bonus to their next action.
    • Hazard: The next time you deal damage to an adversary within your Surveyed Route, add 1d6 physical damage to the damage roll.

    Unused Field Salvage is lost when you take a rest.

Specialization Feature

  • Bad Ground: Once per rest, when an adversary inside your Surveyed Route moves within Close range or farther, you can describe how the terrain turns against them. They must make a Reaction Roll (15).

    On a failure, the adversary becomes temporarily Restrained, or a vehicle becomes Damaged, and they mark 1 Stress. On a success, they mark 1 Stress.

Mastery Feature

  • Dead Zone: Once per long rest, when you create a Surveyed Route, you can turn it into a Dead Zone until the scene ends or you take Severe damage. While inside your Dead Zone:

    • Adversaries that move within Very Close range of you must mark a Stress.
    • Adversaries treat the entire area as difficult terrain.
    • When you deal damage to an adversary in the Dead Zone, add 1d8 physical damage to the damage roll.

ORIGIN QUESTIONS

Answer any of the following origin questions. You can also create your own questions.

  • What did you recover from the ruins that changed your life?

  • Were you raised outside the cities, on the road, or in a place the corps abandoned?

  • What vehicle, route, or piece of salvage do you refuse to abandon?

CONNECTIONS

Ask one of the following questions to your fellow players for their characters to answer, or create your own questions.

  • Which character did you guide through a place that should have killed them?

  • Who trusts your routes more than they trust the map?

  • Which character has seen you risk everything for a vehicle, road, or piece of old tech?

DILEMMAS

Consider the following dilemmas of Humanity and Cyberpsychosis.

  • Humanity: You recover what was lost, guide others through danger, and remember that survival means coming back together.

  • Cyberpsychosis: You begin to see people, places, vehicles, and memories as salvage: useful, broken, replaceable, and worth only what they can become.

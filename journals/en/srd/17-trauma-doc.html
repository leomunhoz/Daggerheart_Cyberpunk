Trauma Docs are field medics, black-clinic surgeons, and cyberware miracle workers who know how to keep people alive when the world has already decided they should be dead.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-classes.Item.{{id:trauma-doc:class}}]{Trauma Doc}

CLASS DETAILS

  • Competency: Medtech

  • Starting Evasion: 10

  • Starting Hit Points: 5

  • Class Items: A trauma kit full of illegal pharmaceuticals and tools, or a bloodstained patient tag from someone you could not save.

HOPE FEATURE

Stay With Me: Spend 3 Hope when a creature within Close range would mark their last unmarked Hit Point or make a Death Move. They don’t take the consequences of the Death Move and remain alive and conscious, clearing 1 Hit Point instead, but become temporarily Vulnerable until they receive medical attention or the scene ends.

CLASS FEATURES

Emergency Medicine: When you have a moment to treat a creature within Melee range, make an Interface Roll (12). On a success, choose one:

  • The target clears 1 Stress.
  • The target clears a temporary physical condition, such as Vulnerable or Restrained.
  • The target stabilizes a damaged implant, life-support system, or cyberware component until their next rest.

Once per rest, when you succeed on this roll, the target can clear 1 Hit Point in addition to choosing one of the effects above. A creature can clear a Hit Point from Emergency Medicine only once per rest.

SUBCLASSES

Choose between Combat Medic or Street Surgeon.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:trauma-doc:Combat Medic:subclass}}]{Combat Medic}

Play as a Combat Medic if you want to keep the crew alive and perform emergency treatment in the middle of combat.

Interface Trait: Instinct

Foundation Feature

  • Rapid Response: When an ally within Close range marks one or more Hit Points, you can mark a Stress to immediately move to within Melee range of them. The next time you use Emergency Medicine on that ally before the scene ends, you roll with advantage.

  • Field Extraction: When you successfully use Emergency Medicine on a creature, you can immediately move them within Very Close range. This movement can place them behind cover, out of immediate danger, or away from enemy vision.

Specialization Feature

  • Mass Casualty Protocol: Once per rest, when you successfully use Emergency Medicine, you can also treat a second creature within Very Close range of the original target.

Mastery Feature

  • Combat Triage: Once per long rest, when a dangerous scene begins or when an ally within Far range marks one or more Hit Points, you can call a triage order. Choose up to three allies within Far range who can hear you, see you, or receive your signal. Each chosen ally may immediately move within Very Close range, then choose one:

    • Clear 1 Hit Point and 1 Stress.
    • Clear a temporary physical condition.
    • Gain a +2 bonus to their next roll.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:trauma-doc:Street Surgeon:subclass}}]{Street Surgeon}

Play as a Street Surgeon if you want to use illegal medicine, cyberware patches, black-clinic procedures, and dangerous stimulants to push bodies past safe limits.

Interface Trait: Knowledge

Foundation Feature

  • Black-Clinic Procedure: When you successfully use Emergency Medicine on a creature, you can give them a dangerous enhancement in addition to the normal effect. Choose one:

    • They add 1d8 to their next damage roll.
    • They gain a +2 bonus to their next Agility, Strength, or Finesse Roll.
    • They gain a +2 bonus to Evasion against the next attack that targets them.

    After the enhanced roll or effect resolves, they mark 1 Stress.

Specialization Feature

  • Bad Medicine: Once per rest, when you succeed on a Medtech card, weapon attack, or Interface Roll against an adversary within Close range, you can turn your medical expertise against them.

    The target marks 1 Stress. The next time that target deals damage, reduce the damage by 1 Hit Point.

Mastery Feature

  • Miracle Cocktail: Once per long rest, when you successfully use Emergency Medicine on a creature, you can flood their body with illegal stabilizers, combat drugs, nerve blockers, or emergency cyberware commands.

    In addition to the normal benefits of Emergency Medicine, the target may immediately move within Close range and make one action roll with advantage. If this action deals damage, add 2d8 to the damage roll.

ORIGIN QUESTIONS

Answer any of the following origin questions. You can also create your own questions.

  • Who was the first person you failed to save?

  • What procedure did you perform that should have been impossible?

  • What illegal drug, implant, or technique do you know far too well?

CONNECTIONS

Ask one of the following questions to your fellow players for their characters to answer, or create your own questions.

  • Who refuses to follow your medical instructions?

  • Which character knows what you do when there are not enough supplies for everyone?

  • Which character did you patch up after a job they still will not talk about?

DILEMMAS

Consider the following dilemmas of Humanity and Cyberpsychosis.

  • Humanity: You know every body is a person, every wound has a story, and survival means more than keeping someone breathing.

  • Cyberpsychosis: You begin to see pain as data, bodies as systems, and consent as an obstacle when the procedure is necessary.

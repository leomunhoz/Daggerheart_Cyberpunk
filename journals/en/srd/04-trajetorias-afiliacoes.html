STEP 3: CHOOSE YOUR LIFE PATH AND AFFILIATION

In Edgeheart, characters are not defined by ancestry or bloodline. They are shaped by the systems that made, broke, trained, bought, abandoned, and rebuilt them.

At character creation, choose one Life Path and one Affiliation. They take the places of Ancestry and Community on the sheet.

CHOOSING A LIFE PATH

A Life Path represents where you came from and what kind of life shaped you before you became a mercenary. A Life Path tells you what the world did to you.

You can always rename, reskin, or rewrite a Life Path to better fit your character.

You can also combine two Life Paths by choosing the top feature from one and the bottom feature from the other. Work with your GM to best define your character.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-lifepaths.Item.{{id:lifepath:Criação Corporativa}}]{Corpo-Raised}

You were raised inside corporate structure. You understand polished violence, contracts, etiquette, artificial comfort, and the language of people who believe they own the future.

  • Corporate Design: Choose one of your Experiences tied to corporate training, education, etiquette, security, finance, or bureaucracy. Gain a permanent +1 bonus to it.

  • Efficient Routine: When you take a short rest, you can choose one long rest move instead of one short rest move.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-lifepaths.Item.{{id:lifepath:Nascido nas Ruas}}]{Streetborn}

You grew up in the streets, alleys, and gang districts of the New Dark Age. You learned how to read danger before it speaks.

  • Alley Reflexes: When you make an Agility Roll, you can spend 2 Hope to reroll your Hope Die.

  • Cheap Shot: When you succeed on an attack against a target within Very Close range using an improvised weapon, hidden blade, dirty trick, or street tactic, you can spend a Hope to make them temporarily Vulnerable.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-lifepaths.Item.{{id:lifepath:Nômade}}]{Nomad}

You come from road clans, convoy families, or people who survived by never staying owned. You know engines, routes, and the value of family chosen at road.

  • Road Sprint: When you succeed on an Agility Roll or vehicle roll to move from Far or Very Far range into Melee range with one or more targets, you can mark a Stress to deal 1d12 physical damage to all targets within Melee range.

  • Off-Road Born: You can move naturally through rubble, broken roads, mud, sand, shallow water, wreckage, and unstable ground.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-lifepaths.Item.{{id:lifepath:Excedente Militar}}]{Military Surplus}

You were trained by a corporate army, gang warband, or collapsed national force. Maybe you deserted. Maybe your unit was sold. Maybe you are the only one left.

  • Combat Reactions: Mark a Stress to gain advantage on a Reaction Roll.

  • Last One Standing: When you have 1 Hit Point remaining, attacks against you have disadvantage.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-lifepaths.Item.{{id:lifepath:Sobrevivente de Clínica Clandestina}}]{Black-Clinic Survivor}

You were rebuilt in an illegal clinic, experimental trauma ward, chop shop, organ market, cyberware den, or emergency surgical pit. Your body carries the price of survival.

  • Scar Tissue: When you would mark a Hit Point or Stress from physical damage, roll a d6. On a 6, don’t mark it.

  • Emergency Rebuild: When you would take Severe damage, you can mark a Stress to mark 1 fewer Hit Point.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-lifepaths.Item.{{id:lifepath:Nativo do Ermo}}]{Wasteland Native}

You come from a dead zone or place the city pretends does not exist. You know that survival is not luck. It is attention.

  • Hazard-Bred: You can breathe, move, and act naturally in smoke, ash, dust, polluted air, shallow water, heavy rain, toxic stink, or unstable terrain unless it is immediately lethal.

  • Hard as Nails: Gain a bonus to your damage thresholds equal to your Proficiency.

CHOOSING AN AFFILIATION

An Affiliation represents the group, community, corporation, gang, or institution you are still connected to. An Affiliation tells you who still has a claim on you.

An Affiliation does not mean total loyalty. You might be a former member, protected outsider, or someone who still owes a debt.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-affiliations.Item.{{id:affiliation:Ativo Corporativo}}]{Corporate Asset}

You are connected to a corporation as an employee, sponsored mercenary, or former insider.

Elite Privilege: You have advantage on rolls to consort with corporate employees, pass as authorized, or leverage your company reputation to get what you want.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-affiliations.Item.{{id:affiliation:Ligado a uma Gangue}}]{Gang-Tied}

You are connected to a gang, street family, or violent local power.

Street Wise: You have advantage on rolls to negotiate with criminals, detect lies, recognize gang signs, or find a safe place to hide in criminal territory.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-affiliations.Item.{{id:affiliation:Comboio Nômade}}]{Nomad Convoy}

You are connected to a convoy, road clan, or wasteland route.

Law of the Road: Record three rules, codes, or protocols your convoy drilled into you. Once per rest, when you describe how you’re embodying one of these principles through your current action, you can roll a d20 as your Hope Die.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-affiliations.Item.{{id:affiliation:Rede de Mercenários}}]{Mercenary Network}

You are connected to a mercenary company, job board, or professional network of hired guns.

Unbound: Once per session, when you make an action roll with Fear, you can change it to a roll with Hope instead.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-affiliations.Item.{{id:affiliation:Clínica Clandestina}}]{Black Clinic}

You are connected to a street doctor, illegal clinic, or illegal cyberware medical ring.

Chillax: During a short rest, you or an ally can reroll a die used for a recovery, repair, or cyberware-related downtime action.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-affiliations.Item.{{id:affiliation:Rede de Fixers}}]{Fixer Network}

You are connected to information sellers, job handlers, and people who know a person who knows a person.

Know the Deal: You have a sixth sense for dealing with the uncertainties of your profession. When you roll with Fear, place a token on this Affiliation. You can hold a number of tokens equal to your level. Before you make an action roll, you can spend any number of these tokens to gain a +1 bonus to the roll for each token spent. At the end of each session, clear all unspent tokens.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-affiliations.Item.{{id:affiliation:Célula de Resistência}}]{Resistance Cell}

You are connected to rebels, labor organizers, or anti-corporate cells.

Found Family: Once per session, you can spend a Hope to use an ally’s Affiliation Feature. When you do, your ally gains a Hope.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-affiliations.Item.{{id:affiliation:Culto de Dados}}]{Data Cult}

You are connected to digital mystics, Old Net scavengers, Blackwall obsessives, rogue archivists, signal prophets, or people who believe the machine is haunted.

Forbidden Pattern: Your connection to corrupted data, rogue AI signs, Blackwall residue, impossible signals, or lost technology might have altered your perception of reality. You can spend 2 Hope to reroll your Fear Die.

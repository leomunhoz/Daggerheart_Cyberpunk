STEP 2: CHOOSE YOUR CLASS

Your Class represents your mercenary specialty within the crew. It is not everything you are. It is what people hire you to do, what keeps you alive under pressure, and what kind of danger you understand better than anyone else.

Each Class grants your core playstyle, your Primary Competency, a Hope Feature, a Class Feature, and Subclasses as you advance.

You can gain access to other Competencies through cyberware, licenses, tools, Eidolons, black-market modifications, and narrative events.

Some Competencies, such as Redline and Blackwall, are not tied to starting Classes. They represent unstable, illegal, or forbidden power that must be acquired through dangerous modifications, corrupted systems, lost technology, or story events.

SPECIAL COMPETENCIES

Redline and Blackwall are not starting Class Competencies. They represent power that should not be normal.

Redline is accessed through illegal overclock implants, rageware, combat drugs, unstable cyberware, dangerous body modifications, and systems that push the user toward cyberpsychosis.

Blackwall is accessed through corrupted AI fragments, forbidden Old Net relics, Black ICE cores, impossible code, rogue intelligence, and technology recovered from beyond the Black Wall.

A character can gain access to these Competencies through cyberware, Eidolons, rare equipment, dangerous contracts, narrative consequences, or GM-approved advancement.

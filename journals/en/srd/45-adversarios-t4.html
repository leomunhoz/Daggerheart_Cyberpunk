TIER 4 — LEGENDS AND GOD-CLASS THREATS

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Cyber Hunter}}]{Cyber Hunter}

A remote-controlled ace pilot and augmented sniper operating three contracts ahead of everyone else.

Motives & Tactics: Kite, mark, execute
Difficulty: 20 | Thresholds: 19/38 | HP: 9 | Stress: 8
ATK: +5 | Rail Spear: Very Far | 4d8+10 phy
Experience: Perfect Kill Angle +5

  • Target Package — Passive: When this adversary damages a PC, that PC becomes Marked until the end of the scene or until they break line of sight for a full Spotlight.

  • Piercing Shot — Passive: Attacks against Marked targets have advantage and ignore cover.

  • Remote Reposition — Action: Mark a Stress to move anywhere within Far range and make a standard attack against a Marked target.

  • Blackout Shot — Action: Mark 2 Stress and attack a Marked target. On a success, deal damage and disable the target’s cyberwares until it marks a Stress to re-enable it.

  • Hold The Breath — Reaction: When this adversary fails an attack roll, mark a Stress to reroll the d20.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Ares-Tyrant Eidolon}}]{Ares-Tyrant Eidolon}

A Deus-Class assault Eidolon built around a war intelligence that does not accept retreat.

Motives & Tactics: Escalate, annihilate, never withdraw
Difficulty: 21 | Thresholds: 50/72 | HP: 14 | Stress: 9
ATK: +5 | Mass Driver: Far | 4d12+11 phy
Experience: Deus-Class Warfare +5

  • Mecha Structure — Passive: This Eidolon adversary reduces damage it would take by half unless the effect comes from another Eidolon.

  • Ares Overdrive — Passive: When this adversary deals Major damage, the target also marks 1 Stress.

  • Thermal Talon — Action: Mark a Stress to attack a target within Melee range. On a success, deal 4d12+10 techno damage. The target is also Overheated and take an extra 2d6 techno damage if they are still Overheated at the end of their action.

  • War God Awakens — Action: Mark 3 Stress. Until the end of the scene, all its attacks gain advantage and +1d12, but any PC who deals damage to it gains 1 Hope.

  • No Retreat — Reaction: When a PC moves away from this adversary, mark a Stress to move within Close range of them and make a reaction standard attack.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Hades Shard}}]{Hades Shard}

A broken fragment of the Deus-Class AI that killed the old internet.

Motives & Tactics: Consume systems, erase identity, birth ghosts
Difficulty: 21 | Thresholds: 20/42 | HP: 10 | Stress: 10
ATK: +5 | Black Signal: Very Far | 4d8+12 techno
Experience: Dead Internet +5

  • Digital Body — Passive: This adversary have immunity against physical damage unless its caused on the device where it is hosted.

  • Digital Afterlife — Passive: When a creature within Far range marks its last Hit Point, Hades creates a Digital Ghost echo of them.

  • Identity Collapse — Action: Mark 2 Stress. One target within Far range must make an Instinct Reaction Roll. On a failure, they mark 2 Stress and cannot use Experiences until their next rest.

  • Ghost Birth — Action: Mark 3 Stress and choose one connected device or cyberware implant within Far range. A Digital Ghost manifestation emerges from it as a temporary threat.

  • Dead Network — Reaction: When a PC creates a Breach within Far range, Hades may mark a Stress to corrupt it. The PC mark 1 Stress.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Board Executive}}]{Board Executive}

A corporate sovereign with lawyers, kill-teams, hostage clauses, and legal ownership of the battlefield.

Motives & Tactics: Buy, threaten, reframe, erase liability
Difficulty: 20 | Thresholds: 18/36 | HP: 10 | Stress: 12
ATK: +4 | Executive Override: Far | 4d6+10 techno
Experience: Corporate Sovereignty +5

  • Liability Shield — Passive: While at least one corporate ally is within Close range, this adversary gains +2 Difficulty.

  • Hostile Acquisition — Action: Mark 2 Stress. One target within Far range must make a Presence Reaction Roll. On a failure, they cannot gain Hope until the end of the scene.

  • Asset Freeze — Action: Mark a Stress and choose one PC. Until the end of the scene, they must mark 1 Stress before using credits, contacts, gear access, a vehicle, or a connected system.

  • Contractual Violence — Reaction: When a PC threates this adversary, mark a Stress to summon two corporate allies of tier 2 or lesser that enters the scene within Close range of it.

  • Plausible Deniability — Reaction: When this adversary would mark HP, redirect the consequence to one corporate ally, bodyguard, hostage, legal shield, decoy, or escape protocol within Close range.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Chrome Reaper}}]{Chrome Reaper}

A legendary full-body cyborg, known to some as the Boogeyman of the New Dark Age, of who remembers being human as a weakness.

Motives & Tactics: Dominate, punish, prove superiority
Difficulty: 20 | Thresholds: 34/68 | HP: 12 | Stress: 8
ATK: +5 | Integrated Arsenal: Far or Melee | 4d10+8 phy
Experience: Living Weapon +5

  • Cyberpsycho — Passive: When this adversary deals damage, the GM gains a Fear.

  • Weapon Switch — Action: Make a standard attack. On a roll of 15+, choose one: push the target to Close range, make them mark 1 Stress, or deal +1d10 damage.

  • Personal Threat — Action: Mark 2 Stress and choose one PC within Far range. Until this PC rolls with Hope, attacks against that PC gain advantage and deal +1d10 damage.

  • Unfair Reflexes — Reaction: When a PC rolls with Hope against this adversary, mark a Stress to change it to a roll with Fear and then immediately move within Close range or make a standard attack.

  • Chrome Supremacy — Reaction: When a PC causes this adversary to mark HP, become Vulnerable, Restrained, or lose sight of a PC, this adversary may mark a Stress to negate this effect and make that PC mark a Stress instead.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Black Hand}}]{Black Hand}

Black Hand is not the best shot in the world, the fastest blade, or the strongest chrome body. He is worse: he is good enough at everything to kill specialists at their own game.

Motives & Tactics: Finish the contract
Difficulty: 20 | Thresholds: 22/44 | HP: 10 | Stress: 8
ATK: +5 | Black Arsenal: Far or Melee | 4d10+8 phy
Experience: Legendary Mercenary +5

  • A Legend Like You — Passive: The first time each scene a PC spends Hope while within Far range of Black Hand, he may immediately make a standard attack against them.

  • Contract Target — Passive: When this adversary damages a PC, that PC becomes his Target until the end of the scene or until another PC deals Major damage to Black Hand. Hands’s attacks against Marked targets deal +1d8 damage.

  • Black Hand Guns — Action: Make a standard attack then choose a weapon: Hand Cannon: The target marks a Stress. Mono-Blade: If the target is within Melee range, they mark two Armor Slot without receiving its benefits. Smart Rifle: The next attack against the target gains +2. Shock Gauntlet: The target becomes temporarily Vulnerable on Major damage.

  • Hard Entry — Reaction: When a PC rolls with Hope against this adversary, mark a Stress to immediately move within Close range, draw a new weapon, and either clear a temporary condition or gain advantage on the next attack.

  • Eidolon Drop — Reaction: When the GM spends 3 Fear after the Black Hand has marked at least 5 HP, it deploys his Eidolon. Clear all temporary conditions from Black Hand, replace his stat block with The Raven Eidolon but keep their marked Stress and Features. Any PCs who were marked as his Target remain marked.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:The Raven Eidolon}}]{The Raven Eidolon}

Black Hand's Eidolon, deployed with Eidolon Drop when the contract gets too heavy. It keeps Black Hand's marked Stress and features.

Difficulty: 21 | Thresholds: 44/66 | HP: 14 | Stress: 8
ATK: +5 | Integrated War Suite: Far or Melee | 4d10+10 phy
Experience: Legendary Eidolon +5

  • Merciless Advance — Action: Mark a Stress and move within Far range, and make a standard attack. If the PC is marked as a Target, this attack has advantage.

  • Killbox Execution — Action: Mark 3 Stress. The Raven floods the area within Far range of it with missiles, suppressive fire, and close-range assault force. Make a standard attack against all PC inside the area. On a success, deal damage and the PC becomes marked as Black Hand’s Target.

  • Black Hand Override — Reaction: When this adversary would fail an attack roll, mark 2 Stress to reroll the d20. If the reroll succeeds, the target also becomes temporarily Vulnerable.

Wardens are bodyguards, riot breakers, and professional protectors who survive by making themselves the hardest problem in the room while they stand between the crew and whatever wants them dead.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-classes.Item.{{id:warden:class}}]{Warden}

CLASS DETAILS

  • Competency: Aegis

  • Starting Evasion: 9

  • Starting Hit Points: 7

  • Class Items: A defensive rig marked by old impacts or a contract token from someone you swore to protect.

HOPE FEATURE

Not Today: Spend 3 Hope when an ally within Close range would mark one or more Hit Points. Move to within Very Close range of that ally and reduce the damage severity by one threshold. If this prevents them from marking any Hit Points, you may also clear 1 Stress or force the attacker to mark 1 Stress.

CLASS FEATURES

Guardian Protocol: At the end of your Spotlight, choose one ally, vehicle, or objective within Close range as your Guarded Asset until your next Spotlight.

The first attack made against your Guarded Asset before your next Spotlight has disadvantage.

If that attack misses, you may immediately move within Very Close range of the attacker or your Guarded Asset, and the attacker marks 1 Stress.

SUBCLASSES

Choose between Bulwark or Riotbreaker.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:warden:Bulwark:subclass}}]{Bulwark}

Play as a Bulwark if you want to become heavy armor, riot shielding, and the person enemies regret hitting.

Interface Trait: Strength

Foundation Feature

  • Impact Frame: Gain a +1 bonus to your Armor Score.

    When you mark an Armor Slot to reduce incoming damage, you may force the attacker to mark 1 Stress or push them back to Close range, the attacker’s choice.

Specialization Feature

  • Hard to Break: Once per rest, when you would mark one or more Hit Points, you can mark a Stress to reduce the severity by one threshold.

Mastery Feature

  • Unbreakable Frame: Once per long rest, when you mark an Armor Slot, you can activate your full defensive rig until the scene ends or you mark your last Armor Slot. While this feature is active, when you mark an Armor Slot, choose one:

    • Clear 1 Stress.
    • Force the attacker to mark 1 Stress.
    • One ally within Very Close range gains a +1 bonus to Evasion until your next Spotlight.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:warden:Riotbreaker:subclass}}]{Riotbreaker}

Play as a Riotbreaker if you want to break charges, control space, shove enemies around, and turn defense into force.

Interface Trait: Presence

Foundation Feature

  • Crowd Control: When you succeed on a weapon attack or Aegis card, choose one adversary within Close range. Until your next Spotlight, that adversary can’t willingly move closer to one ally, objective, or position you choose unless they first mark 1 Stress.

    If the target is already within Very Close range of the protected ally, objective, or position, they must immediately move away to Close range or mark 1 Stress.

Specialization Feature

  • Hard Stop: Once per rest, when an adversary within Close range moves toward you or an ally, force them to make a Reaction Roll (15).

    On a failure, they stop moving and become temporarily Restrained.

    On a success, they mark 1 Stress.

Mastery Feature

  • Break the Line: Once per long rest, when you enter the center of a dangerous fight, choose any number of adversaries within Close range. Each chosen adversary must make a Reaction Roll (16).

    On a failure, they are pushed back to Close range and become temporarily Vulnerable.

    On a success, they mark 1 Stress.

ORIGIN QUESTIONS

Answer any of the following origin questions. You can also create your own questions.

  • Who taught you to put yourself between danger and someone else?

  • Who did you fail to protect?

  • What part of your armor, shield, or defensive rig has saved your life the most?

CONNECTIONS

Ask one of the following questions to your fellow players for their characters to answer, or create your own questions.

  • Which character have you personally carried out of danger?

  • Which character once protected you when you could not protect yourself?

  • Who knows the name of someone you failed to save?

DILEMMAS

Consider the following dilemmas of Humanity and Cyberpsychosis.

  • Humanity: You protect people because lives are not replaceable, even in a world that prices them like equipment.

  • Cyberpsychosis: You begin to see yourself as armor, pain as duty, and your body as something meant to be spent before anyone else falls.

TIER 3 — BLACKSITE AND GHOST WAR

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Sitil Black-Ops Captain}}]{Sitil Black-Ops Captain}

A corporate commander with encrypted orders and permission to erase witnesses.

Motives & Tactics: Command, isolate, execute
Difficulty: 17 | Thresholds: 16/32 | HP: 7 | Stress: 6
ATK: +3 | Command Rifle: Far | 3d8+5 phy
Experience: Tactical Command +4

  • Operational Control — Passive: Corporate allies within Close range gain +1 Difficulty.

  • Kill Order — Action: Mark a Stress. The captain and one corporate ally within Far range immediately moves within Very Close range and makes a standard attack.

  • No Witnesses — Action: Mark 2 Stress and choose one object, body, terminal, witness, camera, or piece of evidence within Far range. It is destroyed, erased, extracted, or placed in immediate danger. If it chooses a target, it becomes Marked. Corporate allies that attacks a Marked target have advantage against it.

  • Asset Denial — Reaction: When a PC rolls with Fear, lock down one door, route, elevator, drone, or security system within Far range.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Rival Edgerunner Crew}}]{Rival Edgerunner Crew}

A full mercenary crew moving with rehearsed roles, inside jokes, and a shared objective.

Motives & Tactics: Outmaneuver, steal objective, cover each other
Difficulty: 16 | Thresholds: 15/30 | HP: 8 | Stress: 6
ATK: +2 | Mixed Loadout: Far | 3d8+8 phy
Experience: Crew Tactics +4

  • Horde (2d6+4) — Passive: When this Horde has marked half or more of its HP, its standard attack deals 2d6+4 physical damage instead.

  • Role Switch — Passive: When this adversary takes the Spotlight, choose one or mark a Stress to choose two: gain +2 Difficulty; deal +1d8 damage; clear 1 Stress.

  • Setpiece Combo — Action: Mark 2 Stress. The crew executes a rehearsed cinematic play: the driver crashes through a barrier, the runner kills the lights, the sniper pins someone down, the bruiser opens a path, or the face creates a distraction. Choose two: 1- The crew immediately moves anywhere within Far range, ignoring difficult terrain, cover, crowds, or minor obstacles; 2- One PC within Far range must succeed on an Agility or Instinct Reaction Roll or mark 2 Stress; 3- The crew’s next attack gains advantage and deals +1d8 damage; 4- A door, route, vehicle, piece of cover, or objective becomes compromised, exposed, blocked, or moved into danger.

  • Cover Each Other — Reaction: When this adversary would mark HP, mark a Stress to reduce the damage severity by one threshold.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Combat Eidolon Frame}}]{Combat Eidolon Frame}

A neural-linked war machine deployed when infantry, drones, and ordinary armor are no longer enough.

Motives & Tactics: Dominate space, complete the mission, reshape the battlefield
Difficulty: 18 | Thresholds: 20/40 | HP: 9 | Stress: 6
ATK: +4 | Integrated Weapon: Far or Melee | 3d10+7 phy
Experience: Eidolon Warfare +4

  • Mecha Structure — Passive: This Eidolon adversary reduces damage it would take by half unless the effect comes from another Eidolon.

  • Frame Pattern — Passive: When this Eidolon enters the scene, choose one frame pattern: Assault Frame: Its standard attacks deal +1d10 damage. Guardian Frame: The Eidolon and any allies of it within Close range gains +1 Difficulty.

  • Battlefield Tactics — Action: Mark a Stress and choose one: Suppress: Attack a target within Far range. On a success, it cannot voluntarily mark Armor Slot, Stress or spent Hope until it rolls with Hope. Ram: Attack a target within Melee range. On a success, the target is pushed back to Close range, marks 1 Stress and becomes temporarily Restrained. Breach Path: Destroy, open, cross, or force through one obstacle within Far range.

  • Reactor Surge — Reaction: When this Eidolon marks HP, mark a Stress to immediately choose one: move within Far range, clear a temporary condition, or give its next attack advantage.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Data Cult Oracle}}]{Data Cult Oracle}

A human body carrying too many machine voices and one prophecy.

Motives & Tactics: Convert, reveal, destabilize

ATK: +2 | Forbidden Signal: Far | 3d6+5 techno
Experience: Blackwall Theology +4

  • Unwanted Revelation — Passive: When a PC rolls with Fear within Close range, they must answer one question the Oracle asks about their fear, desire, or cyberware and then mark a Stress.

  • Convert the Weak — Action: Mark a Stress. A target within Far range must make a Presence Reaction Roll. On a failure, they mark 2 Stress and become temporarily Vulnerable.

  • Cult Transmission — Action: Mark 2 Stress. Until this adversary’s next Spotlight, Oracle’s allies within Close range gain +1 Difficulty and their attacks deal +1d6 techno damage.

  • Signal Martyr — Reaction: When this adversary is defeated, one Digital Ghost within Far range clears 2 Stress and 1 HP.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Digital Wraith}}]{Digital Wraith}

A predatory AI ghost wearing the shape of something the victim almost remembers.

Motives & Tactics: Vanish, possess devices, punish fear
Difficulty: 18 | Thresholds: 13/27 | HP: 6 | Stress: 7
ATK: +3 | Memory Claws: Close | 3d6+6 techno
Experience: Impossible Presence +4

  • Unstable Render — Passive: The Digital Wraith does not occupy space like a physical creature. It appears through AR static, camera feeds, and corrupted sensors. When a PC attacks the Digital Wraith, they must choose one: 1- Mark 1 Stress to lock onto the correct signal or; 2- Make the attack with disadvantage. A PC who has created a Breach against the local network this scene ignores this effect.

  • Possess Device — Action: Mark a Stress to possess a vehicle, device, weapon, or implant within Far range. This adversary may immediately activate, move or attack from it.

  • Fear Echo — Reaction: When a PC rolls with Fear within Far range, they see something personal in the signal and mark 1 Stress.

  • Digital Vanish — Reaction: When this adversary marks HP, it becomes Hidden and reappears through another connected device within Far range.

ATK: +3 | Rotary Cannon: Far | 3d10+4 phy

  • Armor-Piercing Burst — Reaction: When this adversary’s attack succeed against a PC, that PC must marks an Armor Slot without receiving it’s benefits. If the PC cannot mark an Armor Slot, it marks a Stress instead.

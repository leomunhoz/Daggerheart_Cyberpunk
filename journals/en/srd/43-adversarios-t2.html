TIER 2 — PROFESSIONAL VIOLENCE

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Blood Saint Duelist}}]{Blood Saint Duelist}

A stylish street killer who treats every fight like a public performance.

Motives & Tactics: Duel, humiliate, punish weakness
Difficulty: 15 | Thresholds: 10/20 | HP: 5 | Stress: 4
ATK: +3 | Vibroblade: Melee | 2d8+4 phy
Experience: Duelist Reputation +3

  • Witness Me — Passive: If a PC rolls with Fear within Close range of this adversary, the Duelist gains advantage on their next attack.

  • Signature Cut — Action: Mark a Stress to attack a Vulnerable target. On a success, deal damage and the target must mark 1 Stress.

  • Public Humiliation — Reaction: When this adversary deals Major or Severe damage, choose one PC within Close range who saw it happen. They must lose 1 Hope or become Vulnerable.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Korvax Neural Interrogator}}]{Korvax Neural Interrogator}

A corporate mind-tech specialist who turns fears and memory into weapons.

Motives & Tactics: Extract, pressure, isolate
Difficulty: 15 | Thresholds: 8/18 | HP: 5 | Stress: 5
ATK: +2 | Neural Spike: Close | 2d6+4 techno
Experience: Interrogation +3

  • Legal Threat — Passive: PCs within Close range have disadvantage on rolls to lie, negotiate, or conceal information while under direct questioning.

  • Memory Hook — Action: Mark a Stress. One target within Close range must make an Instinct Reaction Roll. On a failure, they mark 2 Stress. On a success, they mark 1 Stress.

  • Pressure File — Reaction: When a PC rolls with Fear on a social, hacking, or investigation roll, reveal that Korvax already has leverage on them, their crew, their contacts, or their cyberware. The PC marks 1 Stress.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Corporate Response Team}}]{Corporate Response Team}

A disciplined tactical unit moving under drone overwatch and helmet-to-helmet command.

Motives & Tactics: Breach, suppress, secure assets
Difficulty: 14 | Thresholds: 11/22 | HP: 6 | Stress: 4
ATK: +2 | Tactical Rifles: Far | 2d8+4 phy
Experience: Breach Formation +3

  • Horde (1d6+2) — Passive: When this Horde has marked half or more of its HP, its standard attack deals 1d6+2 physical damage instead.

  • Stack Up — Action: Mark a Stress to move within Close range and attack. On a success, the target is pushed back to Close range or marks 1 Stress.

  • Tactical Sweep — Reaction: When a PC rolls with Fear while Hidden, behind cover, or inside a secured area, this Horde immediately identifies their position and one corporate ally gains advantage against them.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Black-Clinic Butcher}}]{Black-Clinic Butcher}

A back-alley surgeon turned combat freak, carrying tools meant for bodies that are still screaming.

Motives & Tactics: Harvest, experiment, disable
Difficulty: 14 | Thresholds: 14/26 | HP: 7 | Stress: 4
ATK: +1 | Bone Saw: Melee | 2d10+3 phy
Experience: Illegal Surgery +3

  • Pain Map — Passive: When this adversary deals damage to a target who has marked HP, that target also marks 1 Stress.

  • Surgical Disable — Action: Mark a Stress to attack a target within Melee range. On a success, deal damage and temporarily disable a cyberware of the target.

  • Emergency Stitching — Reaction: When this adversary would mark HP, they may mark 2 Stress instead.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Contract Sniper}}]{Contract Sniper}

A patient killer hired to end the job before the crew even reaches the door.

Motives & Tactics: Aim, relocate, remove leaders
Difficulty: 15 | Thresholds: 8/16 | HP: 5 | Stress: 3
ATK: +3 | Long Rifle: Very Far | 2d8+5 phy
Experience: Kill Lane +3

  • Prepared Position — Passive: This adversary starts a scene Hidden. The first time this adversary attacks while Hidden, add +1d8 damage.

  • Marked Shot — Action: Mark a Stress and choose one target within Very Far range. The next attack this adversary makes against that target gains a +2 bonus to attack roll.

  • Relocate — Reaction: When a PC rolls with Fear against this adversary, the Sniper becomes Hidden if there’s no PC within Very Close range of it.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-adversaries.Actor.{{id:adversary:Signal Haunt}}]{Signal Haunt}

A corrupted AI presence flickering through cameras, implants, headlights, and dead screens.

Motives & Tactics: Distort senses, isolate, whisper
Difficulty: 15 | Thresholds: 9/18 | HP: 5 | Stress: 5
ATK: +2 | Static Spike: Far | 2d6+5 techno
Experience: Sensor Corruption +3

  • Augmented Haunting — Passive: PCs using cyberware, connected gear, or sensors within Close range have disadvantage on rolls to distinguish real threats from false signals.

  • Signal Spike — Action: Mark a Stress and choose one PC within Far range using connected gear, cyberware, sensors, or comms. They must mark 1 Stress or lose the benefit of that system until they spent a Hope to re-enable it.

  • Hostile Overlay — Reaction: When a PC within Far range rolls with Fear, mark a Stress to corrupt their interface. Their vision or sensors fills with hostile markers and false warnings. They become Vulnerable until they mark 1 Stress to tear through the signal.

Solos are professional combatants, hired guns, assault specialists, and battlefield killers who survive by ending threats before they become problems. They are the mercenaries called when the job turns loud.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-classes.Item.{{id:solo:class}}]{Solo}

CLASS DETAILS

  • Competency: Assault

  • Starting Evasion: 11

  • Starting Hit Points: 6

  • Class Items: A customized weapon with a name or a damaged dog tag from the fight that made your reputation.

HOPE FEATURE

Fire Superiority: Spend 3 Hope after you succeed on a weapon attack. Choose one:

  • Add +2d6 to the damage roll.
  • Choose another target within range that your attack roll would also succeed against.
  • The target marks 1 Stress and becomes temporarily Vulnerable.

CLASS FEATURES

Kill Chain: At the beginning of each scene, your Kill Chain Die is a d4. When you succeed on a weapon attack or Assault card that deals damage, roll your Kill Chain Die and add its result to the damage roll.

If the attack deals Major or Severe damage, increase your Kill Chain Die by one step, from d4 up to d20.

If you fail an attack roll, take a rest, or the scene ends, reset your Kill Chain Die to d4.

SUBCLASSES

Choose between Deadeye or Shock Trooper.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:solo:Deadeye:subclass}}]{Deadeye}

Play as a Deadeye if you want to eliminate priority targets, exploit openings, and make every shot count.

Interface Trait: Finesse

Foundation Feature

  • Marked Target: Once per rest, when you deal damage to a target, you can mark them as your priority target until the scene ends or you mark another target this way.

    When you roll your Kill Chain Die against your marked target, you can reroll the die and use either result.

Specialization Feature

  • Called Shot: When you make a weapon attack against your marked target, you can mark a Stress before the roll to gain a +2 bonus to the attack roll.

    If the attack succeeds and deals any damage, increase your Kill Chain Die by one step, even if the attack did not deal Major or Severe damage.

Mastery Feature

  • Kill Confirmed: Once per long rest, when you deal Severe damage to your marked target, they must mark an additional Hit Point.

    If this defeats them, you can immediately mark a new target as your marked target and set your Kill Chain Die to d10.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:solo:Shock Trooper:subclass}}]{Shock Trooper}

Play as a Shock Trooper if you want to breach fortified positions, fight up close, break enemy lines, and keep moving through danger.

Interface Trait: Strength

Foundation Feature

  • Breach Momentum: When your Kill Chain Die increases, gain a +1 bonus to your Evasion until your next action.

  • Hard Entry: When you make an attack immediately after moving into Melee or Very Close range of your target, you can mark a Stress to add your Kill Chain Die to the damage roll again.

Specialization Feature

  • Clear the Line: Once per rest, when you deal Major or Severe damage to an adversary within Melee or Very Close range, you can force them and each adversary within Very Close range of them to mark 1 Stress.

    Then choose one affected adversary and make them temporarily Vulnerable.

Mastery Feature

  • No Way Back: Once per long rest, when you enter a dangerous fight, set your Kill Chain Die to d10.

    Until the scene ends or you take Severe damage, your Kill Chain Die does not reset when you fail an attack roll.

    While this feature is active, you can’t willingly move away from all adversaries unless an ally is in immediate danger.

ORIGIN QUESTIONS

Answer any of the following origin questions. You can also create your own questions.

  • What weapon do you trust more than most people?

  • What battle, contract, or massacre made your name known?

  • What kind of fight still scares you?

CONNECTIONS

Ask one of the following questions to your fellow players for their characters to answer, or create your own questions.

  • Who pulled you back from going too far?

  • Who do you trust to stand beside you when the job turns loud?

  • Which character once saved you after a fight you should not have survived?

DILEMMAS

Consider the following dilemmas of Humanity and Cyberpsychosis.

  • Humanity: You know violence has consequences, and you still choose when not to pull the trigger.

  • Cyberpsychosis: You begin to measure every problem by range, cover, ammunition, and acceptable casualties.

Brokers are negotiators, public faces, and social predators who understand that power rarely sits in the hands of the person holding the gun. They know who pays, who can be bought, and who needs to disappear before the next contract begins.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-classes.Item.{{id:broker:class}}]{Broker}

CLASS DETAILS

  • Competency: Influence

  • Starting Evasion: 10

  • Starting Hit Points: 5

  • Class Items: A contact list worth more than your bank balance or a contract chip containing a big and illegal corporate deal.

HOPE FEATURE

Fine Print: Spend 3 Hope after you or an ally within Far range fails a Presence Roll. Reveal a secret, bribe, threat, or perfect interruption. The failure becomes a success with Fear.

If the failed roll was made directly against a creature, they also mark 1 Stress.

CLASS FEATURES

Terms of Engagement: When you succeed on a Presence Roll or Influence card against a creature or group, choose one:

  • Pressure: One affected target marks 1 Stress.
  • Opening: The next action roll made by you or an ally against one affected target gains a +2 bonus.
  • Reassurance: You or an ally within Close range clears 1 Stress.

On a success with Hope, you can mark a Stress to choose one additional option.

SUBCLASSES

Choose between Fixer or Icon.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:broker:Fixer:subclass}}]{Fixer}

Play as a Fixer if you want to arrange jobs, call in favors, and navigate the city’s hidden economy.

Interface Trait: Presence

Foundation Feature

  • Black-Market Network: Once per rest in a populated area, declare that you know a contact who can provide restricted gear, shelter, transportation, information, or access to someone difficult to reach. The GM tells you what they want in return.

    When you or an ally uses the contact’s help, gain +2 to one relevant action roll.

Specialization Feature

  • Contacts Everywhere: Once per rest, call in immediate help from a contact. Describe how the contact intervenes and choose one:

    • Reduce the Hit Points marked by you or an ally by 1.
    • Gain +3 to your next action roll.
    • Add 2d8 to your next damage roll.

Mastery Feature

  • Everybody Owes Somebody: Once per long rest, ask the GM who in the scene can be bought, pressured, or turned. Your first Presence Roll or Influence card against them has advantage.

    On a success, choose two options from Terms of Engagement instead of one.

@UUID[Compendium.edgeheart-cyberpunk.edgeheart-subclasses.Item.{{id:broker:Icon:subclass}}]{Icon}

Play as an Icon if you want to weaponize reputation, performance, fame, fear, propaganda, and public perception.

Interface Trait: Presence

Foundation Feature

  • Public Persona: Choose what your name means on the street:

    • Beloved: When you use Terms of Engagement to let yourself or an ally clear Stress, that target clears 2 Stress instead.
    • Feared: When you use Terms of Engagement to make a target mark Stress, you can mark a Stress to make them temporarily Vulnerable.
    • Famous: When you use Terms of Engagement to grant a +2 bonus to a roll, grant a +4 bonus to the roll instead.

    You can change your Public Persona when you take a long rest and explain how your image shifts.

Specialization Feature

  • Live Feed: Once per rest, when you make a successful Presence Roll or use an Influence card while being watched by a crowd, recorded, or streamed by a group, you can amplify the moment.

    Choose any number of creatures within Close range who can see or hear you. Allies clear 1 Stress. Adversaries must mark 1 Stress.

Mastery Feature

  • Living Brand: Once per long rest, you can step fully into your reputation until the scene ends or you take Severe damage. While this feature is active, the first time each Spotlight that you succeed on a Presence Roll or with an Influence card, choose one:

    • An ally who can see or hear you gains a Hope.
    • An adversary who can see or hear you marks 1 Stress.
    • You clear 1 Stress.

ORIGIN QUESTIONS

Answer any of the following origin questions. You can also create your own questions.

  • Who gave you your first real contact?

  • Which faction owes you, and which faction wants you dead?

  • What secret are you keeping because it is more valuable unsold?

CONNECTIONS

Ask one of the following questions to your fellow players for their characters to answer, or create your own questions.

  • Which character did you recruit, rescue, hire, or buy out of trouble?

  • Who knows when you are lying?

  • Who has a debt to you that neither of you talks about?

DILEMMAS

Consider the following dilemmas of Humanity and Cyberpsychosis.

  • Humanity: You use leverage to protect the crew, expose predators, and give people choices they would not have had.

  • Cyberpsychosis: You begin to see every relationship as currency, every secret as ammunition, and every person as a future transaction.

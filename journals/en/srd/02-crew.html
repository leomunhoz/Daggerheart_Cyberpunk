STEP 1: BUILD THE CREW

Edgeheart revolves around a party mechanic called The Crew.

Your Crew is the group, operation, and identity that connects the characters. It might be a mercenary squad, street gang, nomad convoy, resistance cell, black-clinic guard, corporate strike team, or a loose group held together by one dangerous contract.

The Crew helps establish what kinds of jobs the campaign follows, what resources the characters share, and what they are known for in the New Dark Age.

Your Crew is defined by the following details:

  • Name: What do people call you?

  • Reputation: What are you known for?

  • Code: What rule does the Crew refuse to break?

  • Fixer: Who finds contracts and connects you to the city?

  • Home Base: Where do you meet, repair, argue, and recover between jobs?

Crew Assets

Vehicles, safehouses, contacts, equipment, contract rewards, and relics recovered during play can become Crew Assets.

Crew Assets belong to the group rather than one character. They remain available when characters retire, disappear, or die, unless they are lost through the fiction.

When a new character joins the Crew, they begin at the lowest level available for the current Tier:

Tier 1 → Level 1 | Tier 2 → Level 2
Tier 3 → Level 5 | Tier 4 → Level 8

Crew Reputation

Choose a short phrase that describes what your Crew is known for. This becomes a shared Experience called your Reputation, beginning at +2. Examples include:

Never Leave Anyone Behind, Ghosts in Chrome Shells, Sexiest Crew Around, No Corp Owns Us, Fight Under Fire, Blackwall Survivors, Choom Before Money, or The Job Gets Done.

Once per scene, one character can spend a Hope to add the Crew’s Reputation to an appropriate roll. The Reputation increases by +1 whenever the Crew enters a new Tier.

THE EDGE

The Crew shares a resource called Edge, representing teamwork, trust, momentum, and desperate improvisation.

The Crew can hold up to 3 Edge. Once per scene, the Crew gains 1 Edge when:

  • A Tag Team Roll succeeds with Hope.

  • A character marks Hit Points, Stress, or accepts a serious consequence to protect another Crew member.

  • A character places the Crew above their own safety or reward.

  • The Crew completes an important part of the Contract through cooperation.

Crew Edge Moves

Anyone can spend Edge, but the Crew should agree on its use.

Teamwork — 0 Edge
The first time each scene a character Helps an Ally, their advantage die is a d8 instead of a d6. Additionally, when a character starts a Teamwork Action Roll, the characters may divide the Hope spent.

Back Me Up — 1 Edge
When a Crew member fails an action roll, another Crew member may immediately intervene. They move within Close range and make a relevant reaction roll describing how they salvage the situation. On a success, treat the original roll as a success with Fear instead.

Get Them Out — 1 Edge
When a Crew member within Close range would mark Hit Points, become temporarily Vulnerable or Restrained, another character may immediately move within Very Close range of them. Reduce the Hit Points marked by 1 or clear one temporary condition affecting them. Then move both characters within Close range toward cover, a vehicle, an exit, or a safer position.

Plan B — 2 Edge
Reveal a reasonable contingency the Crew prepared for the Contract. The contingency must be plausible, but does not need to have been declared earlier.

  • Hidden Cache: Gain three Supply tokens. Spend one to produce a common consumable, weapon, or tool.

  • Inside Access: Create a Breach against the local network or security system. The next roll made to exploit it gains +4.

  • Sabotage Package: Disable one connected system until the GM spends 2 Fear to restore it.

  • Escape Vector: Establish an extraction route within Far range. The group can leave the scene without another roll.

All In — 3 Edge
Two Crew members may immediately make a Tag Team Roll without spending Hope, even if they have already made one during this session. After the action resolves, each participating character may clear 1 Stress.

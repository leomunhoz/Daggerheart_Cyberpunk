An Eidolon is not a vehicle, an armor, or a weapon. It is a powerful robotic war machine often used by mercenaries.

Most people will never see one. Most who do are already dead.

Eidolons are rare military relics, prototype combat frames, neural-linked machines, and AI-assisted war bodies recovered from blacksites, dead battlefields, and the ruins of the old world. They require a special neural bond commonly called Soul Bond.

When a pilot synchronizes with an Eidolon, they connect their nervous system directly to the frame. The pilot feels its weight, recoil, pain, balance, and damage as if it were their own body.

An Eidolon is treated as Special Cyberware. It does not count as a normal vehicle, armor, weapon, or ordinary cyberware.

When you deploy an Eidolon, it replaces your active HP, Armor, and weapons, while your Hope, Stress, Experiences, and class features remain your own.

An Eidolon should usually grant a unique feature, one or more built-in weapons (that use your Interface trait), armor, and one or more extra Competency Card Access (in addition to your normal loadout Competency cards).

This means Eidolons are powerful, but every deployment pushes the pilot closer to Cyberpsychosis.

SOUL BOND

A Soul Bond, the common name for the HumanPlus procedure or Neuro-Activator Machinery (NAM), is the neural, psychological, and "spiritual" connection between a pilot and an Eidolon.

Most Eidolons only accept one bonded pilot at a time. Establishing a Soul Bond usually requires surgery, calibration, blacksite code, a recovered frame key, an AI imprint, or a dangerous synchronization "ritual".

A pilot can attempt to use an Eidolon without a Soul Bond, but doing so is extremely dangerous. When you synchronize with an Eidolon you are not bonded to, mark 2 Stress and roll your Humanity Die. If the result is equal to or lower than your total Cyber Load, the synchronization fails or the GM introduces a major consequence.

DEPLOYING AN EIDOLON

You can only deploy an Eidolon when the fiction allows it. Eidolons require open space, battlefield access, or enough time to reach and activate the frame.

Besides, deploying an Eidolon attracts attention. Corporations notice. Gangs scatter. Civilians run.

While synchronized, the Eidolon becomes your active body. Your normal weapons, armor, vehicles, and body-based cyberware do not apply while synchronized unless the GM agrees they can interface with the frame.

SYNC COST

Every Eidolon has a Sync Cost. While synchronized with an Eidolon, add its Sync Cost to your total Cyber Load. This Sync Cost is not permanent: it only applies while you are synchronized with the Eidolon.

If you roll your Humanity Die while synchronized, use your total Cyber Load including the Eidolon’s Sync Cost.

EIDOLON DAMAGE

When an Eidolon takes damage, mark Hit Points on the Eidolon, not on the pilot. If a feature, adversary, or narrative consequence specifically targets the pilot inside the frame, the GM may call for the pilot to mark Stress or make a Reaction Roll.

When an Eidolon marks its last unmarked Hit Point, it becomes Disabled: it can’t move, attack, or use its features until it is repaired or reactivated.

When your Eidolon becomes Disabled, choose one:

  • Stay Inside: You remain inside the Disabled frame. You are protected from immediate outside danger.

  • Hard Disconnect: You desynchronize violently to be extracted from the frame. Mark 2 Stress and roll your Humanity Die. If the result is equal to or lower than your total Cyber Load, you suffer a Cyberpsychosis trigger.

  • Emergency Overclock: You reactivate the Eidolon in an Overclock state. Whenever the Eidolon would mark Hit Points, you mark those Hit Points instead. Additionally, at the end of each of your actions, you mark one Hit Point.

REPAIRING AN EIDOLON

Eidolon Armor Slots can be cleared during a rest if the pilot or an ally has tools, parts, and time to repair the frame. The GM may require a roll if the frame is badly damaged, corrupted, missing parts, or being repaired in hostile conditions.

EIDOLON SCALE

Eidolons are built for threats too large, fast, armored, or dangerous for ordinary equipment.

When an Eidolon damages ordinary structures, vehicles, or non-Eidolon machinery, treat the damage as one severity higher.

When ordinary small arms, civilian weapons, or low-grade threats damage an Eidolon, treat the damage as one severity lower.

This does not apply against elite adversaries, anti-armor weapons, military vehicles, other Eidolons, Blackwall entities, or anything built to fight at Eidolon scale.

COMPETENCY LINK

Each Eidolon includes a Competency Link. This represents the frame’s systems, AI imprint, or Theoi-derived machine logic.

A Competency Link is only active while synchronized with the Eidolon. If the Link grants Card Access or a special Charge mechanic, it applies only while using that Eidolon.

A pilot can still use their normal class features and Competency cards while synchronized, but any card that clearly relies on human-scale movement, handheld tools, normal weapons, or the pilot’s physical body may need to be adapted through the frame or may not apply.
